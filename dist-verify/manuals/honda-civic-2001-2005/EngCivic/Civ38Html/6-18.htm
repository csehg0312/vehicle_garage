Cylinder Head

Cam Chain Installation (cont'd)

 6-18  

  1. Install the cam chain guide B.

  1. Remove the pin from the auto-tensioner.

  1. Check the chain case oil seal for damage. If the oil seal is damaged, replace the chain case oil seal (see page 6-23).

  1. Remove old liquid gasket from the chain case mating surfaces, bolts and bolt holes.
  1. Clean and dry the chain case mating surfaces.
  1. Apply liquid gasket, P/N 08C70-K0234M, 08C70-K0334M or 08C70-X0331S, evenly to the cylinder block mating surface of the chain case and to the inner threads of the holes.

  1. Apply liquid gasket to the cylinder block upper surface contact areas (A) on the chain case.
  1. Apply liquid gasket, P/N 08C70-K0334M or 08C70-X0331S, evenly to the oil pan mating surface of the chain case and to the inner threads of the holes.

    NOTE: Do not install the parts if 5 minutes or more have elapsed since applying liquid gasket. Instead, reapply liquid gasket after removing old residue.
