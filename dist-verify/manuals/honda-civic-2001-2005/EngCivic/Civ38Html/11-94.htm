Knock Sensor Replacement

  1. Disconnect the IAT sensor 2P connector.
  1. Remove the clip (A) and the IAT sensor (B).

  1. Install the part in the reverse order of removal.

  1. Remove the intake manifold bracket (A).

  1. Disconnect the knock sensor 1P connector.
  1. Remove the knock sensor (B).
  1. Install the part in the reverse order of removal.
