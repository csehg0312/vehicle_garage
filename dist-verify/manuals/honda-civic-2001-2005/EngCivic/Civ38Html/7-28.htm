Oil Pan Installation

  1. Install the baffle plate.

  1. Install the oil pump (see page 8-12).
  1. Install the oil pan (see page 7-28).
  1. Install the cylinder head (see page 6-42).
  1. Install the transmission (see page 13-10).
  1. Install the engine assembly (see page 5-10).

  1. Remove old liquid gasket from the oil pan mating surfaces, bolts, and bolt holes.
  1. Clean and dry the oil pan mating surfaces.
  1. Apply liquid gasket, P/N 08C70-K0334M or 08C70-X0331S, evenly to the cylinder block mating surface of the oil pan and to the inner threads of the bolt holes.

    NOTE: Do not install the parts if 5 minutes or more have elapsed since applying liquid gasket. Instead, reapply liquid gasket after removing old residue.

  1. Install the oil pan.
  1. Tighten the bolts in two or three steps. In the final step, tighten all bolts, in sequence, to 12 Nm (1.2 kgf/m, 8.7 lbf/ft).
