Reverse Lockout Solenoid Test

Reverse Lockout Solenoid Assembly Disassembly/Reassembly

  1. Remove the reverse lockout solenoid (see page 13-22).
  1. Connect battery positive terminal to the No. 2 terminal of the reverse lockout solenoid 2P connector, and connect the battery negative terminal to the No. 1 terminal.

REVERSE LOCKOUT SOLENOID 2P CONNECTOR

Terminal side of male terminals

  1. Check that the reverse lockout solenoid operates.
  1. If the reverse lockout solenoid does not work, replace it.

  1. Remove the reverse lockout solenoid (see page 13-22).
  1. Remove the roller (A), the select lock return spring (C), and the select lock cam B.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
