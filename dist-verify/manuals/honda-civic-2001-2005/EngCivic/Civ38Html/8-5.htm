Engine Lubrication

Engine Oil Replacement

 8-5  

  1. Warm up the engine.
  1. Remove the drain bolt (A) and drain the engine oil.

  1. Reinstall the drain bolt with a new washer (B).
  1. Refill with the recommended oil (see page 3-2).

    Capacity

4.5 l (4.8 US qt, 4.0 lmp qt) at oil change.

4.7 l (5.0 US qt, 4.1 lmp qt) at oil change including filter.

5.8 l (6.1 US qt, 5.1 lmp qt) after engine overhaul.

  1. Run the engine for more than 3 minutes, then check for oil leakage.
