Clutch Disc and Pressure Plate Installation

  1. Install the ring gear holder.

  1. Apply Urea Grease UM264 (P/N 41211-PY5-305) to the splines (A) of the clutch disc (B), then install the clutch disc using the special tools.
  1. Install the pressure plate (A) and the mounting bolts (B) finger-tight.

  1. Torque the mounting bolts in a criss-cross pattern. Tighten the bolts in several steps to prevent warping the diaphragm spring.

    PRESSURE PLATE MOUNTING BOLT TORQUE:

25 Nm (2.6 kgf/m, 19 lbf/ft)

  1. Remove the special tools.
  1. Make sure the diaphragm spring fingers are all the same height.
