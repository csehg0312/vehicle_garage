Manual Transmission

Transmission Removal (cont'd)

 13-6  

  1. Carefully remove the slave cylinder so as not to bend the clutch line. Do not operate the clutch pedal once the slave cylinder has been removed.

  1. Remove the harness cover (A), water pipe mounting bolts (B) and loosen the air cleaner housing mounting bracket bolt (C). Lower the water pipe slightly.

  1. Attach the engine hanger (D) and the collar (E) to the engine using the 8 x 1.25 mm bolt (F).
  1. Attach the chain hoist to the engine hanger.

  1. Remove the two upper transmission mounting bolts.

  1. Remove the transmission mount bracket (A) and transmission mounting bolt (B).

  1. Remove the air cleaner bracket (C).
