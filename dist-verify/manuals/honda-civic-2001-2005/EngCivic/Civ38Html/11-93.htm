TDC Sensor Replacement

  1. Remove the air cleaner (see page 11-126).
  1. Disconnect the ECT sensor 2P connector.
  1. Remove the ECT sensor (A).

  1. Install the part in the reverse order of removal with a new O-ring (B).

  1. Remove the air cleaner (see page 11-126).
  1. Disconnect the TDC sensor 3P connector.
  1. Remove the TDC sensor (A).

  1. Install the part in the reverse order of removal with a new O-ring (B).
