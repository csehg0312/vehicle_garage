Timing Pulleys Installation

Fuel Supply Pump Pulley Removal

  1. Lock fuel supply pump pulley using TDC fixing bolt (A), then remove the fuel supply pump pulley fixing nut.

  1. Use special tool 5-8840-7002-0 to remove the fuel supply pump pulley (B).

Camshaft Pulley Removal

  1. Lock the camshaft pulley with TDC fixing bolt (A).

  1. Remove the camshaft pulley fixing bolt (B).
  1. Remove the camshaft pulley (C).

Special Tool Required

Crankshaft pulley fixing wrench 5-8840-7008-0

Front Dust Cover Installation

  1. Install the front dust cover (A) and tighten the bolts (B), (C).

    Tightening torque: 9.8 Nm (1.0 kgf/m, 7.2 lbf/ft)

Crankshaft Pulley Installation

  1. Insert the flange (A) to face the flange limb towards pulley side, then install the crankshaft pulley (B).
