Manual Transmission

Transmission Removal (cont'd)

 13-9  

  1. Place the transmission jack under the transmission and remove the transmission rear mount (A).

  1. Remove the transmission mounting bolts.

  1. Pull the transmission (A) away from the engine until the transmission mainshaft clears the clutch pressure plate, then lower transmission on the transmission jack.

  1. Remove the extension plate (B) and two dowel pins (C).
  1. Remove the release bearing and release lever (see page 12-12).
