EPS Components

Steering Gearbox Installation (cont'd)

 17-23  

  1. Install the engine wire harness stay (A) on the bulkhead and fit the engine wire harness on the stay.

  1. Under the steering gearbox, install the ground cable terminal (A) on the gearbox housing.

  1. Install the EPS wire harness stay (A) on the gearbox housing and connect the 6P connector (B).

  1. Centre the steering rack within its stroke and install the left (RHD: right) tie-rod end (A).

  1. Wipe off any grease contamination from the ball joint tapered section and threads. Then reconnect the tie-rod end (A) to the damper steering arms. Install the ball joint nut (B) and tighten it.
  1. Install the new cotter pin (C) and bend it as shown (D) or (E).
