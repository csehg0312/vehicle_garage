Steering Gearbox Installation (cont'd)

 17-22  

  1. Install the pinion shaft grommet (A).

  1. Install the gearbox mounting bracket (A) on the frame.

  1. Slip the left side of the steering gearbox over the mounting stud on the gearbox mounting bracket.
  1. Install the washer (A) and the gearbox attaching nut (B) and lightly tighten.

  1. Insert the pinion shaft up through the bulkhead and install the washers (A) and gearbox attaching bolts (B) and tighten to the specified torque. Then tighten the right side of the gearbox attaching nut to the specified torque value.
