Engine Block

Flywheel Removal and Installation

 7-4

Crankshaft End Play Inspection

Special Tools Required

Stopper 5-8840-7007-0

  1. Lock the flywheel with the special tool and remove the fastening bolts.

  1. Apply Locktite to the top thread of the new fastening bolts.

  1. Install the flywheel and the fastening bolts then lock the flywheel with the stopper.
  1. Tighten the fastening bolts in numerical order as shown in the illustration.

    NOTE: The installation time including the torque check is max. 10 min.

  1. Set the dial indicator as shown in the illustration and measure the crankshaft thrust clearance.

  1. If the thrust clearance exceeds the specified limit, replace the thrust bearing as a set.

    Crankshaft end play:

Standard (New):

0.05 – 0.15 mm (0.002 – 0.006 in.)

Service Limit:

0.2 mm (0.008 in.)
