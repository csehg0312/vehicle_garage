Fuel Supply System

Injector Replacement

 11-159  

Special tool required

Injector fixing tool 5-86751-052-0

Injector removal

  1. Remove the cylinder head cover and loosen bolts (A).

  1. Loosen bolts; bracket fixing (B).
  1. Loosen bolt; bracket fixing (C).
  1. Remove bracket (D).
  1. Remove retainer; injector (E).
  1. Remove injector assembly (F).

Injector installation

  1. Put the bracket (A) onto the bolt; carrier fixing (B).

  1. Insert the injectors (C) into the cylinder head.
  1. Fix the injector angle by the injector fixing tool.

  1. Put the retainer, injector (A) onto slit on the bracket (B).
  1. Put the bracket (C), apply engine oil to bolt (D) and tighten temporary.

    Temporary tightening torque:

2 Nm (0.2 kgf/m, 1 lbf/ft)
