Manual Transmission

Transmission Removal

 13-5  

NOTE: Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.

  1. Write down the frequencies for the radio' s preset buttons. Disconnect the negative (-) cable first, then the positive (+) cable from the battery. Remove the battery.
  1. Remove the intake air duct (see step 6 on page 5-2).
  1. Remove the air cleaner housing (see step 33 on page 5-15).
  1. Disconnect the ground cable (A), then remove the battery tray (B).

  1. Carefully remove the slave cylinder so as not to bent the clutch line. Do not operate the clutch pedal once the slave cylinder has been removed.

  1. Disconnect the back-up light switch connector (A), then remove the water pipe bolt (B).

  1. Disconnect the cables (A) from the cable bracket (B). Carefully remove both cables so as not to bend the cables.
