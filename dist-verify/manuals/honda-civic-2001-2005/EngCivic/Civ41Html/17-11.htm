EPS Components

Steering Gearbox Removal (cont'd)

 17-11  

  1. Pull on the steering gearbox to free it from the mounting stud (A) on the gearbox mounting bracket.
  1. Remove the gearbox mounting bracket (B).

  1. Lower the steering gearbox and rotate it so the pinion shaft points upward.
  1. Remove the pinion shaft grommet (A) from the top of the torque sensor.

  1. Remove the evaporative emission (EVAP) canister by disconnecting the hoses and remove the canister bracket (A).

  1. Remove the coolant expansion tank (B) from the bulkhead and move it aside. Do not disconnect the hoses.
  1. Open the cable clamp (A) and disconnect the heater valve cable (B). Remove the heater valve (C) from the bulkhead and free it.

  1. Release the coolant hose (D) from the hose holders.
