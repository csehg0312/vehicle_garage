Engine Assembly

Engine Installation (cont'd)

 5-16  

  1. Install the battery cables (A) to the under-hood fuse/relay box, then install the battery cable (B) to the battery positive cable clamp.

  1. Clean the battery posts and cable terminals with sandpaper, then assemble them and apply grease to prevent corrosion.
  1. Check that the transmission shifts into gear smoothly.
  1. Refill the engine with engine oil (see page 8-5).
  1. Refill the transmission with fluid (see page 13-4).
  1. Refill the radiator with engine coolant and bleed air from the cooling system with the heater valve open (see page 10-6).
  1. Inspect the idle speed (see page 11-154).
  1. Check the wheel alignment, refer to the '01 Civic Shop Manual on this CD (see page 18-4).
