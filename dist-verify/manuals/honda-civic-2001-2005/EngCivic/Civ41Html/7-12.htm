Grade B:

78.950 – 78.959 mm

  1. Calculate the cylinder block and the piston clearance

Cylinder block and piston clearance:

Standard:

0.061 – 0.079 mm

(0.0024 – 0.0031 in.)

Disassembly

  1. Remove the snap ring (A) from piston (B).

  1. Push to remove the piston pin (C), then remove the connecting rod (D).
  1. Remove the piston rings (E).
Inspection

  1. Use a micrometer to measure the piston pin outside diameter at several points.

Piston pin outside diameter:

Standard (New):

26.995 – 27.000 mm

(1.0628 – 1.0630 in.)
