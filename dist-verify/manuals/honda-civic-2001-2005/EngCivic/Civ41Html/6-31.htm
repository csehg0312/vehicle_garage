Cylinder Head

Cylinder Head Cover Installation

 6-31  

  1. Clean sealing surfaces and remove liquid gasket residue.
  1. Apply liquid gasket (A) and (B) to sealing surfaces on the camshaft carrier.

    Note: Oil return bore must not be covered with liquid gasket. Install the head cover with new gasket to camshaft carrier and install the upper part of timing cover.

  1. Tighten cylinder head cover with tightening order in the illustration.

Tightening torque:

9.8 Nm (1.0 kgf/m, 7.2 lbf/ft)
