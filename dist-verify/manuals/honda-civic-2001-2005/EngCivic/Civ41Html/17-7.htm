EPS Components

Motor Removal/Installation

 17-7  

Removal

  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery.
  1. Remove the air cleaner (A).

  1. Remove the air cleaner mounting bracket (A) from the bulkhead.

  1. Remove the evaporative emission (EVAP) canister (B) from its bracket and move it aside.

  1. Remove the connector stay (A) on the gearbox housing and disconnect the motor 2P connector (B).

  1. Remove the motor (A) from the gearbox housing.

  1. Remove the O-ring (B) and discard it.
