Cylinder Head Removal

Camshaft Pulley Installation

  1. Install the camshaft pulley (A).

  1. Lock the camshaft pulley with TDC fixing bolt (B).
  1. Tighten the fastening bolt (C) to the specified torque, then remove TDC fixing bolt.

Tightening torque: 64 Nm (6.5 kgf/m, 47 lbf/ft)

NOTE: Lay cylinder head assembly on the wooden block as otherwise glow plugs or valves may be damaged.

  1. Remove the drive belt (see page 4-20).
  1. Remove the timing belt (see page 6-7).
  1. Remove the exhaust manifold (see page 9-4).
  1. Remove the intake manifold (see page 9-2).
  1. Remove the upper radiator hose (A), water hose (B), water pipe mounting bolt (C), vacuum pipe mounting bolt (D) and disconnect the glow plug thermo sensor connector (E).

  1. Remove the water inlet hose and water outlet hose from the thermostat housing (A) and water grow plug harness (B).
