M/T Differential

Backlash Inspection

 13-38

Final Driven Gear/Carrier Replacement

  1. Backlash between side gear (A) and pinion gear (B).

Limit:

0.3 mm (0.0118 in.)

  1. If the backlash is beyond the limit, adjust by replacing thrust washers.

  1. Remove the bolts and the ring gear.
  1. Install the ring gear (A).

  1. Apply LOCTITE 271 to the bolt threads and tighten the bolts (B) in sequence shown.

Torque:

113 Nm (11.5 kgf/m, 83 lbf/ft)

NOTE: When installing bolts, clean the bolt threads. Do not apply oil to the threaded portions.

  1. Apply oil to the mating surfaces of the ring gear and differential case.
