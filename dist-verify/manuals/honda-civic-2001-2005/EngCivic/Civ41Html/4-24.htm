Alternator and Vacuum Pump Overhaul

  1. Drain the engine oil from vacuum pump assembly.

  1. Remove the vacuum pump fixing screws (M6).
  1. Remove the vacuum pump assembly.

  1. Remove the through bolts (M5).
  1. Use screw driver to insert clearance between front cover and stator core to divide front side and rear side.

    NOTE: Do not make damage stator coil by screw driver. If do not drive them, catch the rear cover then hit the rotor shaft by plastic hammer.

  1. Remove the pulley nut.

  1. Remove the pulley.
  1. Remove the front cover assembly.
  1. Remove the rotor assembly.
