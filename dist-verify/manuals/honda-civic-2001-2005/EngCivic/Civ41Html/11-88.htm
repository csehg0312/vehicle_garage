Diesel Engine Management System

DTC Troubleshooting (cont'd)

 11-88  

DTC P1003: Injector Performance Malfunction

  1. Check the ''On Board Diagnostic (OBD) System Check''.

Was the ''OBD System Check'' performed?

YES – Go to step 2.

NO – Go to OBD System Check.

  1. Check the DTC.

Are there other DTCs (beside P1003) being output?

YES – Go to relevant DTC chart.

NO – Go to step 3.

  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the ECM connector.
  1. Remove the cylinder head cover insulator.
  1. Check for the following conditions:
  • Damaged injection pipe.
  • Damaged injector.

Is a problem found?

YES – Replace the injector or injection pipe.

NO – Go to step 7.

  1. Perform injector spray condition check.

Is a problem found?

YES – Clean or replace relevant injector.

NO – Substitute a known-good ECM and recheck. If symptom/indication goes away, replace the original ECM.
