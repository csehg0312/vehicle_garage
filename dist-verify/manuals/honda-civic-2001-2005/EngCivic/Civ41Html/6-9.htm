Cylinder Head

Timing Belt Installation

 6-9  

  1. Align the pin on the crankshaft pulley upward of the cylinder block.
  1. Use M8 bolt to lock the fuel supply pump pulley to cylinder block to align the pump phase.
  1. Use M6 bolt to lock the camshaft pulley on the camshaft carrier through the camshaft lock hole (A).

  1. Move the belt tensioner counterclockwise and tighten to 4.9 Nm temporally.

  1. Install the timing belt with correct routing.

  1. Loose the bolt: tension pulley slowly and touch it to timing belt gently.
  1. After making balance between the timing belt and belt tensioner bolt: tension pulley with specified torque.

    (use suitable tool to prevent movement of the tension pulley).

Specified torque: 49 Nm (5.0 kgf/m, 36.2 lbf/ft)
