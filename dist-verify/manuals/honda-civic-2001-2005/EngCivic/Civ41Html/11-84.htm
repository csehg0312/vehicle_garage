Diesel Engine Management System

DTC Troubleshooting (cont'd)

 11-84  

Is a problem found?

YES – Repair short in injector circuit wire.

NO – Go to step 7.

  1. Measure resistance between the injectors connector terminals No. 1 and No. 2.

Is there about 0.2 – 0.3 &ohm; (At room temperature: about 20°C (68°F))?

YES – Go to step 9.

NO – Replace applicable injector.

  1. Clear all DTCs.

Was a problem complete?

YES – System OK.

NO – Substitute a known-good ECM and recheck. If symptom/indication goes away, replace the original ECM.
