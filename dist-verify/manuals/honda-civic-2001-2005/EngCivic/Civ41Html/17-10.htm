Steering Gearbox Removal (cont'd)

 17-10  

  1. Remove the cotter pin (A) from the tie-rod ball joint nut (B) and loosen the nut.

  1. Separate the tie-rod ball joint and damper steering arm using the special tool.
  1. Grasp the passenger's side tie-rod and pull the rack all the way to the passenger's side (RHD: left direction, LHD: right direction), then remove the driver's side tie-rod end.
  1. Remove the engine wire harness stay (A) from the right side (RHD: left) of the bulkhead.

  1. Remove the steering gearbox attaching nut and washer from right side (RHD: left) of the gearbox.

  1. Remove the steering gearbox attaching bolts and washers from left side (RHD: right) of the gearbox.
