Manual Transmission

Transmission Fluid Inspection and Replacement

 13-4

Back-up Light Switch Test

  1. Park the vehicle on level ground and turn the engine OFF.
  1. Remove the oil filler plug (A) and O-ring (B), check the condition of the fluid and make sure the fluid is at the proper level (C).

  1. If the transmission fluid is dirty, remove the drain plug (D) and drain the fluid.
  1. Reinstall the drain plug with a new O-ring and refill the transmission fluid to the proper level.

    Oil Capacity

2.0 l (2.1 US qt, 1.8 lmp qt)

Always use Genuine Honda Manual Transmission Fluid (MTF). Using motor oil can cause stiffer shifting because it does not contain the proper additives.

  1. Reinstall the oil filler plug with a new O-ring.

  1. Disconnect the back-up light switch (A) connector.

  1. Check for continuity between the back-up light switch connector terminals. There should be continuity when the shift lever is in reverse.
  1. If necessary, replace the back-up light switch. Install the new washer and back-up light switch on the transmission housing.
