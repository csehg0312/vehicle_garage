Cooling System

Radiator Replacement

 10-12  

  1. Drain the engine coolant (see page 10-6).
  1. Disconnect the fan motor connectors (A), hood switch connector (B) and compressor clutch connector (C), then remove the harness clamps (D).

  1. Remove the condenser brackets (A) and the upper bracket and cushions (B), then remove the bulkhead (C).

  1. Remove the intercooler inlet hose (A) and intercooler outlet hose (B).

  1. Remove the upper radiator hose (A), lower radiator hose (B) and water bypass hoses (C).
