Cylinder Head Removal (cont'd)

 6-11  

  1. Support the engine with a jack and wood block under the oil pan.
  2. Remove the upper bracket.

  1. Remove the cover (A), then remove the exhaust manifold (B).
  1. Remove the intake manifold.

  1. Remove the four ignition coils.
  2. Remove the throttle cable clamps (A) and harness holder (B) from the cylinder cover.
