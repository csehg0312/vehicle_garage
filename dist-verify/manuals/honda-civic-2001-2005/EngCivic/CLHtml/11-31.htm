DTC Troubleshooting (cont'd)

 11-31  

  1. Measure voltage between ECM/PCM connector terminal E7 and body ground.

ECM/PCM CONNECTOR E (31P)

Wire side of female terminals

Is there battery voltage?

YES - Go to step 13.

NO - Substitute a known-good ECM/PCM and recheck, refer to the '01 Civic Shop Manual on this CD (see page 11-313). If symptom/indication goes away, replace the original ECM/PCM.

  1. Disconnect ECM/PCM connector A (31P).
  2. Measure voltage between body ground and ECM/PCM connector terminals A3 and A2 individually.

ECM/PCM CONNECTOR A (31P)

Wire side of female terminals

Is there battery voltage?

YES - Go to step 15.

NO - Substitute a known-good ECM/PCM and recheck, refer to the '01 Civic Shop Manual on this CD (see page 11-313). If symptom/indication goes away, replace the original ECM/PCM.

  1. Remove the glove box.
  2. Remove the PGM-FI main relay 1 (A).

  1. Measure voltage between body ground and ECM/PCM connector terminals A3 and A2 individually.

ECM/PCM CONNECTOR A (31P)

Wire side of female terminals

Is there battery voltage?

YES - Repair short to power in the wire between the ECM/PCM (A2, A3) and the PGM-FI main relay 1.

NO - Replace the PGM-FI main relay 1.
