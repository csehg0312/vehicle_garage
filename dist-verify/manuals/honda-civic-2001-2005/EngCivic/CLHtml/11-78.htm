PGM-FI System

MIL Circuit Troubleshooting (cont'd)

 11-78  

  1. Connect ECM/PCM connector terminal E31 and body ground with a jumper wire.

ECM/PCM CONNECTOR E (31P)

Wire side of female terminals

  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

Is the MIL on?

YES - Substitute a known-good ECM/PCM and recheck. If symptom/indication goes away, replace the original ECM/PCM.

NO - Check for an open in the wire between the ECM/PCM (E31) and the gauge assembly. Also check for a blown MIL bulb. If the wires and the bulb are OK, replace the gauge assembly.

  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  2. Inspect the No. 6 ECU (ECM/PCM) (15A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box.

Is the fuse OK?

YES - Go to step 18.

NO - Go to step 12.

  1. Remove the blown No. 6 ECU (ECM/PCM) (15A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.
  1. Remove the PGM-FI main relay 1 (A).

  1. Check for continuity between body ground and the PGM-FI main relay 1 4P connector terminals No. 2 and No. 4 individually.

Wire side of female terminals

Is there continuity?

YES - Repair short in the wire between the No. 6 ECU (ECM/PCM) (15A) fuse and the PGM-FI main relay 1. Also replace the No. 6 ECU (ECM/PCM) (15A) fuse.

NO - Go to step 15.
