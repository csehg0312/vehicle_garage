A/T Interlock System

Shift Lock Solenoid Replacement (cont'd)

 14-93  

  1. Remove the shift assembly (see page 14-79).
  2. Remove the shift lock solenoid connector (2P), then disconnect the connector (see page 14-82).
  3. Remove the D3 switch wire from the hook. The wire routes over the shift lock solenoid.
  4. Pull the lock tab (A) up, then remove the shift lock solenoid (B).

  1. Install the shift lock solenoid plunger (C) and plunger spring (D) in the new shift lock solenoid.
  2. Apply silicone grease the inside of the slot on the shift lock solenoid plunger and tip of the shift lock/reverse lock stop.
  3. Install the shift lock solenoid by aligning the joint of the shift lock solenoid plunger with the tip (E) of the shift lock/reverse lock stop.
  4. Connect the shift lock solenoid connector (2P) and install it to the shift lever bracket base.
  5. Hook the D3 switch wire on its hook.
  6. Install the shift lever assembly (see page 14-80).
