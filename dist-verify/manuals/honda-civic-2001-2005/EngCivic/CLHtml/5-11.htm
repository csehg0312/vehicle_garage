Engine Removal (cont'd)

 5-11  

  1. Make sure the hoist brackets are positioned properly. Raise the hoist to full height.
  2. Remove the rear mount mounting bolts.
  1. Use a marker to make alignment marks on the reference lines (A) that align with the centres of the rear sub-frame mounting bolts (B).

  1. Remove the front sub-frame.
  2. Check that the engine/transmission is completely free of vacuum hoses, fuel and coolant hoses and electrical wiring.
  3. Slowly lower the engine approximately 150 mm (6 in.). Check once again that all hoses and wires are disconnected from the engine/transmission.
  4. Lower the engine all the way. Remove the chain hoist from the engine.
  5. Remove the engine from under the vehicle.
