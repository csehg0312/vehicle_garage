Automatic Transmission

System Description (cont'd)

 14-61  

When the shift lever is shifted to position from position, the shift lock solenoid is turned ON and the shift lock solenoid plunger is retracted to release the reverse lock stop. This allows the shift lever to be moved to position.

In the allowable conditions of turning ON the solenoid are not met, the shift lock solenoid remains OFF and the reverse lock stop locks the shift lever in the position.
