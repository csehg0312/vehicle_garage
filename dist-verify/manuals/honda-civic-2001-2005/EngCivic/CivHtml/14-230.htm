A/T Differential

Carrier Bearing Side Clearance Inspection

 14-230  

Special Tools Required

Driver 40 mm I.D., 07746-0030100

  1. Install the 80 mm set ring (A) in the transmission housing (B).

  1. Install the differential assembly (A) in the torque converter housing (B) using the special tool.
  1. Align the spring pin (A) on the control shaft with the transmission housing groove (B) by turning the control shaft (C).

NOTE: Be careful not to squeeze the end of the control shaft tips together when turning the shaft. If the tips are squeezed together it will cause a faulty signal or position due to the play between the control shaft and the switch.

  1. Install the three dowel pins (D) and the gasket (E) on the torque converter housing (F).

  1. Place the transmission housing (G) on the torque converter housing.
  2. Install the housing bolts along with the transmission hanger (H) and connector bracket (I). Tighten the bolts in two or more steps to 44 Nm (4.5 kgf/m, 33 lbf/ft) in a criss-cross pattern.
