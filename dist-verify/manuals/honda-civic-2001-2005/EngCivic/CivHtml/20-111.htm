Trunk Lid

Trunk Lid Weatherstrip Replacement

 20-111  

  1. Remove the trunk lid weatherstrip (A) by pulling it off.

  1. Forward
  1. Locate the painted alignment mark (B or C) on the trunk lid weatherstrip. Align the painted mark with the alignment tab in the centre of the trunk, and install the weatherstrip. Make sure it's seated completely and facing in the direction shown. Make sure there are no wrinkles in the weatherstrip.
  2. Check for water leaks.
