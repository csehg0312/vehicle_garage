Tailgate

Tailgate Weatherstrip Replacement

 20-250  

  1. Remove the tailgate weatherstrip (A) by pulling out on it.

  1. Locate the painted alignment mark (B) on the tailgate weatherstrip. Align the painted mark with the alignment tab in the centre of the tailgate opening and install the tailgate weatherstrip all the way around in the direction shown. Make sure there are no wrinkles in the weatherstrip.
  2. Check for water leaks.
