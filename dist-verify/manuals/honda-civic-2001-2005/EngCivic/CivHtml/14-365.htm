End Cover/Intermediate Housing

Planetary Carrier Bearing Replacement

 14-365

Manual Valve Body Disassembly, Inspection and Reassembly

Special Tools Required
  • Handle driver, 07749-0010000
  • Driver attachment, 42 x 47 mm, 07746-0010300
  1. Remove the bearing from the planetary carrier.

  1. Install the new bearing until it bottoms in the planetary carrier with the special tools.
  1. Clean all parts thoroughly in solvent or carburettor cleaner and dry with compressed air. Blow out all passages.
  1. Coat all parts with ATF during assembly.

  1. REVERSE INHIBITOR VALVE
  2. REVERSE INHIBITOR VALVE SPRING
  3. VALVE SPRING SEAT
  4. MANUAL VALVE
  5. MANUAL VALVE BODY

    Inspect for wear, scratches and scoring.
