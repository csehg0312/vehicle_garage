Conventional Brake Components

Master Cylinder Disassembly (cont'd)

 19-21  

5-door
  1. Remove the rod seal from the master cylinder (dual brake booster type) or brake booster (single brake booster type).
  2. Remove the hoses (A) from the reservoir (B) and master cylinder (C).

  1. Remove the reservoir seal (A) from the reservoir cap (B).

  1. Remove the strainer (C) from the reservoir (D).
  1. Remove the circlip (A) while pushing in the secondary piston (B).

  1. Remove the reservoir union (A) and remove the grommets (B) from the reservoir union.

NOTE: When reservoir union and master cylinder body are separated, replace the grommets with new ones.

  1. On vehicle's with ABS, remove the stop pin (C) while pushing in the secondary piston (D).
