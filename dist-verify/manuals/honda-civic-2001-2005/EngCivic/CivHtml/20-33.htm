Mirrors

Power Mirror/Manual Mirror Replacement

 20-33  

  1. Lower the door glass fully.
  1. Carefully pry out the mirror mount cover (A) by hand in the sequence shown.
    Fastener Location
    : Clip, 1

  1. On power mirror model: Disconnect the connector (A).
    Fastener Location
    : Nut, 3

  1. While holding the mirror, remove the nuts securing the mirror, and remove the mirror (manual mirror model).
  1. On power mirror model: While holding the mirror, push out the connector clip (A), and remove the mirror (B). Take care not to scratch the door.

  1. Install the mirror in the reverse order of removal, and make sure the connector is plugged in properly (power mirror model).
