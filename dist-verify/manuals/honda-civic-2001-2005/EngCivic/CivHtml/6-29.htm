Cylinder Head

Cylinder Head Removal (cont'd)

 6-29  

  1. Remove the intake resonator.

  1. Disconnect the intake air temperature (IAT) sensor connector (A) and remove the breather hose, then remove the air cleaner housing (B).
  1. Remove the brake booster vacuum hose (A) and positive crankcase ventilation (PCV) hose (B).

  1. Relieve fuel pressure, 4-door (see page 11-129), or 5-door (see page 11-427).
  2. Remove the EVAP canister hose.
