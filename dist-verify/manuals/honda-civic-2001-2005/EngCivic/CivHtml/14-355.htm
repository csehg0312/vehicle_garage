Transmission Lower Valve Body

ATF Pan, ATF Strainer and Lower Valve Body Assembly Removal

 14-355  

  1. Remove the ATF pan (A) (14 bolts).
  1. Remove the ATF strainer (B) (two bolts).
  1. Remove the lower valve body assembly (C) (eight bolts).
  2. Remove the bolt securing the solenoid harness connector (D) with holding the lower valve body assembly and remove the connector and valve body assembly.
  3. Remove the ATF cooler lines (E).
  4. Clean the inlet opening (A) of the ATF strainer (B) thoroughly with compressed air, then check that it is in good condition and that the inlet opening is not clogged.

  1. Test the ATF strainer by pouring clean ATF through the inlet opening and replace it if it is clogged or damaged.
