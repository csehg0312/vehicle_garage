Interior Trim

Carpet Replacement (cont'd)

 20-214  

  1. Remove the front edge of the trunk floor mat (A) (see page 20-208), then remove the rear floor carpet (B)

  1. Install the carpet in the reverse order of removal and note these items:
  • Take care not to damage, wrinkle or twist the carpet.
  • Make sure the seat harnesses are routed correctly.
  • Replace any damaged clips.
  • Reattach the cut areas under the heater with a wire tie.
  • When installing new carpet, cut the carpet (A) under the heater area (B). After installing new carpet, slip the carpet from the driver's and passenger's side through the space between the heater unit and body.
