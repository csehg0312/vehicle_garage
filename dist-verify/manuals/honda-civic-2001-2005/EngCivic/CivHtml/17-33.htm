Pump Overhaul (cont'd)

 17-33  

  1. Align the bolt holes in the cover (B) with the threaded holes in the pump housing. Install the five flange bolts (C) loosely first, then torque the flange bolts in 2 or more steps with sequence shown.

  1. Push in the cam ring (A) from the pump housing cap hole (B) with a flat blade screwdriver, to make sure the cam ring is fully seated against the outer case.
  1. Install the pump preload spring (A) in the pump housing.

  1. Coat the new 12.7 mm O-ring (B) with power steering fluid, and install it on the pump housing cap (C).
  2. Install the pump housing cap (C) on the pump housing, and tighten it to specified torque.
  3. Coat the pressure control valve (A) with power steering fluid, and install pressure control valve and spring (B) on the pump housing.

  1. Coat the new 16.7 mm O-ring (C) with power steering fluid, and install it on the flow control valve cap (D).
  2. Install the flow control valve cap (D) on the pump housing, and tighten it to the specified torque.
