End Cover/Intermediate Housing

Input Shaft and Planetary Carrier Clearance Inspection

 14-364  

  1. Measure the clearance between the 25 x 31 mm thrust shim (A) and the snap ring (B) with a feeler gauge (C).

NOTE: Take measurements in at least 3 places and use the average as the actual clearance.

STANDARD: 0.05-0.115 mm (0.018-0.030 in.)

  1. If the clearance is out of standard, remove the 25 x 31 mm thrust shim (A) and measure its thickness.
  1. Select and install the new thrust shim and secure them with the snap ring, then recheck.
