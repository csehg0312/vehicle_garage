Rear Door Latch Replacement (cont'd)

 20-156  

  1. Disconnect the actuator connector(s) (A) and for some models, detach the harness clips (B) and actuator connector. Release the inner handle rod (C) and lock rod (D) from the rod holder (E) and remove the screws securing the latch (F). Take care not to bend any of the rods.

Fastener Locations
: Bolt, 3

European models:

Except european models:
  1. Disconnect the inner handle rod (A) and lock rod (B) from the latch (C).

  1. Remove the bolt securing the rear lower channel (A).
    Fastener Location
    : Bolt, 1
