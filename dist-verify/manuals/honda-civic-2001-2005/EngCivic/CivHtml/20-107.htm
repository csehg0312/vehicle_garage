Hood Seal Replacement

  1. Check that the hood opens properly and locks securely.
  1. Apply touch-up paint to the hinge mounting bolts and around the hinges.
  2. Grease each location of the hood latch and hood hinge as indicated by the arrows.
  1. Using a clip remover, detach the clips (A, B), then remove the hood seal (C) and hood sub-seal (D). Take care not to scratch the hood.

Fastener Locations
A : Clip, 14 B : Clip, 2

  1. Install the seals in the reverse order of removal, and replace any damaged clips.
