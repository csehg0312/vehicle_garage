A/T Interlock System

Park Pin Switch Replacement

 14-171  

KH Model
  1. Remove the centre console panel and centre console (see page 20-73).
  2. Remove the screws (A), then remove the shift lever knob (B).

  1. Remove the A/T gear position indicator panel light (C) from the A/T gear position indicator panel.
  2. Remove the A/T gear position indicator panel and panel bracket as an assembly (D).
  3. Disconnect the park pin switch connector (4P) (E) and remove it from the shift lever bracket base (F).
  4. Remove the shift lock release (G) and spring (H).
  1. Open the lock tabs (A) on the shift lever bracket base, then remove the park pin switch (B).

  1. Install the new park pin switch.
  2. Install the removed parts in the reverse order of removal.
