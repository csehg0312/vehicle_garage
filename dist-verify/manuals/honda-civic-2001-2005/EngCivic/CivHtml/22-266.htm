Security Horn Test

  1. Open the hood.
  2. Disconnect the 2P connector from the hood switch.

Terminal side of male terminals

  1. Check for continuity between the terminals.
  • There should be continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals when the hood is opened.
    (lever released)
  • There should be no continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals when the hood closed.
    (lever pushed down)
