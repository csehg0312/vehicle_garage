Automatic Transmission

Transmission Removal (cont'd)

 14-124  

  1. Disconnect the mainshaft speed sensor connector (A) and remove the harness clamp (B) from its bracket (C).

  1. Disconnect the VSS connector (A) and remove the harness clamp (B) its bracket (C).

  1. Remove the transmission range switch connector (D) from its bracket (E), disconnect the connector and remove the harness clamp (F) from its bracket (G).
  1. Remove the ATF cooler hoses (A) from the ATF cooler lines (B). Turn the ends of the ATF cooler hoses up to prevent ATF from flowing out, then plug the ATF cooler hoses and lines.

NOTE: Check for any signs of leakage at the hose joints.

  1. Disconnect connectors from the A/T clutch pressure control solenoid valves A and B, shift solenoid valves A and B (C) and mainshaft speed sensor (D) and remove the harness clamps (E) from the clamp brackets (F).
