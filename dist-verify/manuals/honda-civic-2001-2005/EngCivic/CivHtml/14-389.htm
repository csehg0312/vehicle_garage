Honda Multi Matic Transmission/CVT Differential

Oil Seal Replacement

 14-389  

Special Tools Required
  • Handle driver, 07749-0010000
  • Driver attachment, 78 x 80 mm, 07NAD-PX40100
  • Driver attachment, 58 mm, 07JAD-PH80101
  1. Remove the oil seal (A) from the transmission housing (B).

  1. Remove the oil seal (A) from the flywheel housing (B).
  1. Install the oil seal in the transmission housing with the special tools.

  1. Install the oil seal (A) in the torque converter housing with the special tools.
