Fuel Fill Door Opener Cable Replacement

 20-265  

SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations (see page 23-24) and the precautions and procedures (see page 23-25) in the SRS section before performing repairs or service.

NOTE:

  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • care not to scratch the body and related parts.
  1. Remove these items:
  • Front door sill trim, right side (see page 20-206)
  • Rear door sill trim, both sides (see page 20-206)
  • Trunk side trim panel, left side (see page 20-208)
  1. Pull the carpet and rear floor carpet back as necessary (see page 20-212).
  2. Disconnect the fuel fill door opener cable (A) from the opener (B) (see step 3 on page 20-267).
    Fastener Locations
    C : Cushion tape, 5 D : Clip, 1

  1. Remove the cushion tape (C) and detach the clip (D) by using a clip remover.
  2. Remove the fuel fill door latch (E) from the body by turning it 90°.
  3. Remove the fuel fill door opener cable from the vehicle. Take care not to bend the cable.
  4. Install the opener cable in the reverse order of removal and replace the cushion tape and replace the clip if it damaged.
