Conventional Brake Components

Rear Brake Shoes Replacement (cont'd)

 19-41  

  1. Lower the brake shoe assembly and remove the lower return spring (A). Be careful not to damage the dust cover of the wheel cylinder.

  1. Remove the brake shoe assembly from the backing plate and disassemble the shoe assembly.
  2. Disconnect the parking brake cable from the parking brake lever and remove the brake shoe.
  3. Remove the U-clip (A), wavy washer (B) and pivot pin (C) and separate the parking brake lever (D) from the brake shoe (E).

 Reassembly (5-door)
  1. Apply rubber grease (Solvest 113) on to the sliding surfaces as illustrated. Wipe off any excess. Do not get grease on the brake linings.
    Sliding surfaces

  1. Install the parking brake lever (A) to the backward brake shoe (B) and secure with the pivot pin (C), wavy washer (D) and a new U-clip (E). Pinch the U-clip securely to prevent from coming off.

NOTE: Install the wavy washer with its convex side facing outside.

  1. Connect the parking brake cable to the backward brake shoe.
