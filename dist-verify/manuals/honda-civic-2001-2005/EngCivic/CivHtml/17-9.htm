Steering Column Removal and Installation

 17-9  

SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations (see page 23-23), precautions and procedures (see page 23-25) in the SRS section before performing repairs or service.

Removal

  1. Disconnect the negative cable form the battery.
  2. Remove the driver's airbag and the steering wheel (see page 17-6).
  3. Remove the column covers (A).

  1. Remove the combination switch assembly (B) from the steering column shaft by loosening a screw (C) on the top of the combination switch.
  2. Disconnect the 2P, 6P, and 7P connectors from the ignition switch.
  3. Disconnect the steering joint (D), and remove it from the column shaft.
  4. Remove the steering column (E) by removing the attaching nuts and bolts.
