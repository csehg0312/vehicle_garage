Dashboard

Steering Hanger Beam Replacement (cont'd)

 20-227  

  1. Remove the bolts (A) and screws (B) securing the dashboard (C) and steering hanger beam (D) and disconnect the power mirror switch connector (E). On auto A/C model, disconnect the in-car temperature sensor connector (F). Separate the dashboard and steering hanger beam.

Fastener Locations
A : Bolt, 4 B : Screw. 8

  1. Install the beam in the reverse order of removal and note these items:
  • Make sure the dashboard wire harness (G) is not pinched.
  • Make sure the connectors are connected properly.
