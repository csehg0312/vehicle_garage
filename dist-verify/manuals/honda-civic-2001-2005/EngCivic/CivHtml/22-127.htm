Turn Signal/Hazard Flasher

Hazard Warning Switch Test

 22-127  

4-door:
  1. Remove the centre panel (see page 20-78).
  2. Disconnect the 10P connector (A) from the hazard warning switch (B).

  1. Push out the hazard warning switch from behind the centre panel (C).
  2. Check for continuity between the terminals in each switch position according to the table.

*: KM and KP models

 5-door:
  1. Remove the shift lever panel (see page 20-220).
  2. Disconnect the 10P connector (A) from the hazard warning switch (B).

  1. Remove the two screws and the hazard warning switch.
  2. Check for continuity between the terminals in each switch position according to the table.
