Exterior Lights

High Mount Brake Light Replacement

 22-120  

4-door
  1. Open the trunk lid and disconnect the 2P connector (A) from the high mount brake light (B).
    High Mount Brake Light: 21 W

  1. Carefully remove the rear shelf (see page 20-65) and the high mount brake light.
  2. Install the light in the reverse order of removal.

 5-door
  1. Open the tailgate and remove the tailgate upper trim (see page 20-209).
  2. Remove the two mounting bolts from the high mount brake light (A).

  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (B) from the light.
  2. Remove the screw and separate the lens (A) and housing (B).
    High Mount Brake Light: 5 W x 5

  1. Clean the rear window glass and install the light in the reverse order of removal.
