DTC Troubleshooting - with Side Airbags (cont'd)

 23-135  

  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  2. Disconnect the F3o connector (A) from the under-dash fuse/relay box.

  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  2. Check for voltage between the No. 3 terminal of the O1i connector and body ground. There should be 0.5 V or less.

O1i CONNECTOR

Wire side of female terminals

Is the voltage as specified?

YES - Faulty OPDS unit; replace the OPDS unit.

NO - Go to step 16.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  2. Disconnect the C2 connector (A) from the gauge assembly.

  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  2. Check for voltage between the No. 3 terminal of the O1i connector and body ground. There should be 0.5 V or less.

O1i CONNECTOR

Wire side of female terminals

Is the voltage as specified?

YES - Short to power in the gauge assembly; replace the gauge assembly.

NO - Go to step 20.
