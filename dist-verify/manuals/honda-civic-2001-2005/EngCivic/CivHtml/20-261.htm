Fuel Pipe Protector Replacement

For Some Model
  1. Remove the screws, then remove the rear strake (A). Take care not to scratch the body.
    Fastener Locations
    : Screw, 2

  1. Install the strake in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Remove the clips, then remove the fuel pipe protector (A). Take care not to scratch the body.

Fastener Locations
: Clip, 4

  1. Install the protector in the reverse order of removal and replace any damaged clips.
