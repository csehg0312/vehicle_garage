Mirrors

Mirror Holder Replacement - Large Type Mirror

 20-172  

  1. From under the mirror, remove the hole seal (A) and remove the screw (B).

  1. Pull out the mirror holder (A) and actuator (B) by releasing the hooks (C).

  1. Record the wire colours and connecting positions of the terminals (D) and disconnect the terminals from the actuator, then remove the mirror holder with the actuator.
  1. Install the mirror holder in the reverse order of removal and note these items:
  • Make sure the terminals are plugged in properly.
  • Check the operation of the actuator.
