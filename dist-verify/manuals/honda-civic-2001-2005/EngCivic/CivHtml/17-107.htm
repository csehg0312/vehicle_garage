EPS Components

Steering Gearbox Installation (cont'd)

 17-107  

  1. Under the steering gearbox, install the ground cable terminal (A) on the gearbox housing.

  1. Install the engine wire harness bracket (A) on the bulkhead.
  1. Install the driver's side tie-rod end.
  1. Wipe off any grease contamination from the ball joint tapered section and threads. Then reconnect the tie-rod end (A) to the damper steering arms. Install the 10 mm nut (B) and tighten it.

  1. Install the new cotter pin (C), and bend it as shown.
  2. For RHD vehicle's with CVT; connect the shift cable to the transmission (see step 31 on page 14-336). For RHD vehicle's with M/T; install the clutch reservoir and reservoir bracket (see page 12-6). For LHD vehicle's with M/T; connect the shift cable to the transmission (see step 18 on page 13-12).
