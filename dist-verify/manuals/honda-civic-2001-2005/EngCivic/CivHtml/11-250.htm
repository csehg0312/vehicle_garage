PGM-FI System

DTC Troubleshooting (cont'd)

 11-250  

  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the VTEC solenoid valve 2P connector.
  2. Check for resistance between the VTEC solenoid valve 2P connector terminal No. 2 and body ground.

VTEC SOLENOID VALVE 2P CONNECTOR

Terminal side of male terminals

Is there 14-30 ohms?

YES

- Go to step 10.

NO

- Replace the VTEC solenoid valve.

ECM/PCM CONNECTOR B (24P)

Wire side of female terminals

Is there continuity?

YES

- Repair short in the wire between the VTEC solenoid valve and the ECM/PCM (B1).

NO

- Go to step 12.
