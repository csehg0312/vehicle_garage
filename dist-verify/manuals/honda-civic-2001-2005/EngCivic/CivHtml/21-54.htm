Blower Unit Removal and Installation

 21-54  

4-door

NOTE: LHD type is shown, RHD type is symmetrical.

  1. Remove the passenger's dashboard lower cover (see page 20-78), the right kick panel (see page 20-64) and the glove box (see page 20-79).
  2. Remove the bolt, then cut the plastic cross brace in the glove box opening with diagonal cutters in the area shown. Remove and discard the plastic cross brace.

  1. Remove the relays (A), then remove the bolts and the glove box frame (B).
  1. Remove the ECM/PCM (see page 11-3).
  1. Disconnect the connectors (A) from the blower motor, the power transistor and the recirculation control motor, then remove the wire harness clips (B).

  1. Fold the carpet and pad back toward you. Remove the mounting bolts, the mounting nut and the blower unit.

  1. Install the unit in the reverse order of removal. Make sure that there is no air leakage.
