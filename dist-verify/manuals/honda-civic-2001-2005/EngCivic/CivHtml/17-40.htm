NOTE: Refer to the Exploded View as needed during this procedure.

Removal

  1. Remove the steering gearbox (see page 17-35).

Disassembly

  1. Unbend the lock washer (A).
  1. Hold the bracket (A) with one wrench, and unscrew both rack ends (B) with another wrench. Remove the lock washers.

  1. Remove cylinder lines (A) from the gearbox.

  1. Drain the fluid from the cylinder fittings by slowly moving the steering rack back and forth.
