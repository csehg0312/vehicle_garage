Honda Multi Matic Transmission/CVT

Transmission Removal (cont'd)

 14-327  

  1. Disconnect the secondary HO2S connector (A) and remove the secondary HO2S harness (B) from its clamps (C).

  1. Remove exhaust pipe A.
  1. Remove the engine stiffeners (A) and flywheel cover (B).

  1. Remove the eight drive plate bolts (C) one at a time while rotating the crankshaft pulley.
  2. Remove the rear mount bolts.
