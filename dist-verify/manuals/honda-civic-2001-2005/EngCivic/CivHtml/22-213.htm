Stereo Sound System

Speaker Replacement

 22-213  

Door Speaker:
  1. Remove the door panel (see page 20-152).
  2. Pull the top of the speaker (A) straight out to release the upper clip, then lift the speaker straight up to release the lower clips.

Front Door:

Rear Door:

  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (B) and remove the speaker.

 Rear (4-door):
  1. Remove the rear shelf (see page 20-65).
  2. Remove the three mounting screws, then disconnect the 2P connector (A) from the speaker (B).
