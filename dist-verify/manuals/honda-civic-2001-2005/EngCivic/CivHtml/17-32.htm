Power Steering

Pump Overhaul (cont'd)

 17-32  

  1. Align the slot (A) in the outer case with the roll pin hole (B) on the cover.

  1. Install the cam ring (C) by aligning the slot (D) with the slot (A) in the outer case.
  2. Insert the roll pin (E) into the slots between the cam ring and outer case, then push roll pin the set hole.
  3. Install the rotor (A) in the cam ring (B).

  1. Set the 11 vanes (C) in the grooves in the rotor. Make sure that the round ends (D) of the vanes are in contact with the sliding surface of the cam ring.
  1. Place the side plate (A) on the cam ring with its ''." mark (B) facing upward, and aligning the roll pin set hole (C) in the side plate with the roll pin end (D).

  1. Coat the new O-ring (E) with the power steering fluid, then position it into the groove on the side plate.
  2. Install the pump housing (A) over the cover assembly (B).
