Power Steering

Steering Gearbox Removal (cont'd)

 17-37  

  1. Remove the engine wire harness (A) from the harness bracket (B).

  1. Remove the steering rack guard (C) by removing the three clips.
  2. Remove the steering gearbox attaching nut and washer on the right gearbox mounting bracket.
  1. Remove the steering gearbox attaching bolts and washers on the left gearbox mounting bracket.

  1. Pull on the steering gearbox to free it from the mounting stud (A) on the right gearbox mounting bracket (B). Remove the three mounting bolts and bracket.
