Auto-Tensioner Removal/Installation (cont'd)

 6-27  

  1. Align the holes on the tensioner pulley (A) and tensioner base (B), then insert 3.0 mm (0.12 in.) diameter pin (C) into the holes.

  1. Install the tensioner spring (A) on the auto-tensioner (B).
  1. Install the auto-tensioner, then tighten the auto-tensioner mounting bolt (A) to 9.8 Nm (1.0 kgf/m, 7.2 lbf/ft).

  1. Loosen the auto-tensioner mounting bolt 180o.
  2. Install the timing belt in a counter-clockwise sequence, starting with the drive pulley.
  1. Drive pulley (A).
  2. Tensioner pulley (B).
  3. Water pump pulley (C).
  4. Camshaft pulley (D).
