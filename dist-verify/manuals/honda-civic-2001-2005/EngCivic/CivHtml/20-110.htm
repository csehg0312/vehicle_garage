Trunk Lid

Trunk Lid Torsion Bar Replacement

 20-110  

Special Tools Required

Torsion bar assembly tool, 07XAZ-S0K0100

  1. Remove the torsion bars (A) from the torsion bar centre clip (B).

  1. Put on gloves to protect your hands. Remove the torsion bars with the torsion bar assembly tool (A) from both trunk lid hinges. First remove the right torsion bar (B), then remove the left torsion bar (C).
  1. Remove the torsion bar centre clip from the body.

  1. Install the torsion bars in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
  • The shapes of the right torsion bar (A) and left torsion bar (B) are shown. Install the torsion bars properly.
  • Adjust the torsion bars forward or rearward with the torsion bar assembly tool.
  • Make sure the trunk lid opens properly and locks securely.
