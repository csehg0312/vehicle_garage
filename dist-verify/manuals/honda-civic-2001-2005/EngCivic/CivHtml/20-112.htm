Fuel Fill Door

Adjustment

 20-112  

  1. Slightly loosen the hinge mounting bolts (A).

  1. Adjust the fuel fill door (B) in or out until it's flush with the body, and up or down as necessary to equalise the gaps.
  2. Tighten the hinge mounting bolts.
  3. Check that the fuel fill door opens properly and locks securely, and check that the rear of the door is flush with the body.
