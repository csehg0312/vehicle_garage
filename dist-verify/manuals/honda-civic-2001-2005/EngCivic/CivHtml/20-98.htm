Rear Seat Armrest Replacement – Fixed Type

 20-98  

For some models:

NOTE: Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.

  1. Remove the seat-back (see page 20-96).
  2. Remove the screws, and release the hooks (A), then remove the trunk pass-through cover (B).
    Fastener Locations
    A : Screw, 2

  1. Remove the bolts, then remove the armrest (A).

Fastener Locations
: Bolt, 4

  1. Remove the beverage holder (A) from the armrest (B).

  1. Install the armrest in the reverse order of removal.
