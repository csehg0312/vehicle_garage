Honda Multi Matic Transmission/CVT

Transmission Installation (cont'd)

 14-333  

  1. Support the sub-frame (A) with a 4 x 4 x 40 in. piece of wood (B) and lift it up to the body.

  1. Loosely install the four sub-frame mounting bolts (A) and the 10 x 1.25 mm bolts (B).
  1. Align the reference marks (A) with the centre of the bolts heads (B), then tighten the bolts to the specified torque.

  1. Install the rear mount bolts.

  1. Remove the transmission jack from the transmission.
