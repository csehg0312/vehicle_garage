Shafts and Clutches

Clutch Disassembly

 14-205  

Special Tools Required
  • Clutch spring compressor set, 07LAE-PX40000
    • Clutch spring compressor attachment, 07LAE-PX40100
    • Clutch spring compressor attachment, 07HAE-PL50101
    • Clutch spring compressor bolt assembly, 07GAE-PG40200
  • Snap ring pliers, 07LGC-0010100
  1. Remove the snap ring (A), then remove the clutch end plate, the clutch discs and the plates with a screwdriver (B).

  1. Remove the wave spring from the 1st, 3rd and 4th clutches.
  1. Remove the disc spring from the 1st-hold clutch (7-position).

  1. Install the special tool on the clutch assembly.
