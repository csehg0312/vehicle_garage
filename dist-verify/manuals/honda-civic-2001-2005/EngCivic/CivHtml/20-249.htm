Tailgate Support Strut Replacement

  1. Tighten each bolt and nut securely.
  1. Check that the tailgate opens properly and locks securely.
  2. Reinstall the support struts securely.
  3. Reinstall all remaining removed parts.
  4. Grease the pivot portion of the tailgate hinges (A) as indicated by the arrows.
  1. With the help of an assistant, remove the pivot bolts (A) from the body and tailgate, then remove the support strut (B). Take care not to scratch the body and tailgate.

  1. If necessary, remove the pivot cap (A), then remove the pivot housing (B) and pivot bolt (C) from the support strut (D).

  1. Install the support strut in the reverse order of removal and before reinstalling the pivot cap (A) and pivot housing (B), replace them with new ones.
