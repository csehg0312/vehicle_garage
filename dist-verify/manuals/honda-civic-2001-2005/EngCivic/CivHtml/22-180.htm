Wiper Motor Replacement (cont'd)

 22-180  

Windshield Wiper Motor (5-door):
  1. Open the hood. Remove the nut covers, nuts (A) and the windshield wiper arms (B).

  1. Remove the hood seals (C) and cowl covers (D).
  2. Disconnect the 5P connector (A) from the wiper motor.

  1. Remove the three bolts (B) and wiper linkage assembly (C).
  1. Scribe a line (A) across the link and windshield wiper linkage to show the original adjustment.
    Separate the windshield wiper linkage (B) from the wiper motor (C).
