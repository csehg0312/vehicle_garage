Front and Rear Door Glass Adjustment

For Some Models
  1. With a Torx T40 bit, remove the door hook pin (A) from the door.

  1. With a Torx T40 bit, remove the bolts, then remove the door catch (A) from the body.
    Fastener Locations
    : Bolt, 2

  1. Install the hook pin and catch in the reverse order of removal, and apply liquid thread lock to the threads of the door hook pin.

 NOTE: Check the weatherstrips and glass run channel for damage or deterioration, and replace them if necessary.

  • Plastic cover, front door (see page 20-3), rear door (see page 20-5)
