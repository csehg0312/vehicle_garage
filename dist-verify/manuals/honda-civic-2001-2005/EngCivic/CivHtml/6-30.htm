Cylinder Head Removal (cont'd)

 6-30  

  1. Remove the ground cable (A), upper radiator hose (B), lower radiator hose (C), heater hose (D) and water bypass hose (E).

  1. Remove the two bolts securing the connecting pipe, then remove the connecting pipe from the water passage.
  1. Remove the adjusting plate mounting bolt (A), locknut (B) and mounting bolt (C), then remove the power steering (P/S) pump belt (D) and pump without disconnecting the P/S hoses.

  1. Remove the alternator (see page 4-31).
  2. Remove the air conditioning (A/C) hose bracket (A) and P/S hose bracket (B).
