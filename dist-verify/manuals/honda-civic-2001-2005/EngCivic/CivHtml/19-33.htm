Conventional Brake Components

Brake Booster Replacement

 19-33  

4-door
  1. Remove the master cylinder (see page 19-18).
  2. Disconnect the vacuum hose (A) from the brake booster.

  1. Remove the air conditioner hose (B) and power steering hose (C) from their respective holders.
  2. Remove the flange bolts (D) and throttle cable holder (E).
  3. Remove the brake pipes (A) from the ABS modulator (B).
  1. Remove the under-dash cover and fuse/relay box in the passenger compartment.
  1. Remove the clip (A) and the joint pin (B) and disconnect the yoke from the brake pedal.

  1. Remove the brake booster mounting flange nuts (C).
  2. Remove the brake booster (A) from the engine compartment.

  1. Install the brake booster in the reverse order of removal and note these items:
  • Adjust the pushrod clearance before installing the brake booster (see page 19-29).
  • Use a new clip whenever installing.
  • After installing the brake booster and master cylinder, fill the reservoir with new brake fluid, bleed the brake system (see page 19-7), and adjust the brake pedal height and free play (see page 19-5).
