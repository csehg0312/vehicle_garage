Exterior Lights

Headlight Adjuster Switch Test

 22-116  

4-door:
  1. Remove the driver's pocket (see page 20-76).
  2. Disconnect the 5 P connector (A) from the headlight adjuster switch (B), then remove the switch.

  1. Measure resistance between the No. 1 and No. 3 terminals and No. 2 and No. 3 terminals at positions 0, 1, 2, and 3 by moving the switch knob.

Between the No. 1 and No. 3 terminals:

About 4.7 k ohms

Between the No. 2 and No. 3 terminals:

Knob position

0 1 2 3
Resistance

[About (k ohms)]

 0.7 1.7 2.0 2.6

5-door:
  1. Remove the driver's pocket (see page 20-216).
  2. Carefully push out the headlight adjuster switch (A) from behind the dashboard.

  1. Disconnect the 5P connector (B) from the switch.
  2. Measure resistance between the No. 1 and No. 3 terminals and No. 1 and No. 2 terminals at positions 0, 1, 2, and 3 by moving the switch knob.

Between the No. 1 and No. 3 terminals:

About 4.7 k ohms

Between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals:

Knob position

0 1 2 3
Resistance

[About (k ohms)]

 0.7 1.4 1.9 2.4
