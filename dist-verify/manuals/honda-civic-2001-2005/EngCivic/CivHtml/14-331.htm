Honda Multi Matic Transmission/CVT

Transmission Installation

 14-331  

  1. Remove the used grease in the flywheel hub cap and flywheel splines.
  1. Push out Super High Temp Urea Grease (P/N 41211-PY5-305) in weight about 2.5 grams (0.086 oz.) using a ruler. Length of 40 mm (1.57 in.) pushed out grease weighs 1 gram (0.035 oz.)

  1. Fill the inside of the flywheel hub cap (A) with measured Super High Temp Urea Grease. Do not fill over 2.5 grams (0.086 oz.), it may damage the transmission.
  1. Install the front mount on the flywheel housing.

  1. Install the starter (A) on the flywheel housing (B).

  1. Install a new rubber sealing ring (C) on the input shaft (D) and install the 14 mm dowel pins (E) in the flywheel housing.
  2. Install the flywheel (F) on the input shaft.
  3. Place the transmission on a jack and raise it to the engine level.
