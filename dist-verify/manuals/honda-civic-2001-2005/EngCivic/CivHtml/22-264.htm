Security System

Door Key Cylinder Switch Test

 22-264  

4-door:
  1. Remove the door panel (see page 20-7).
  2. Disconnect the 3P connector from the key cylinder switch.

Wire side of female terminals

  1. Check for continuity between the terminals.
  • There should be continuity between the No. 2 and No. 3 terminals when the door key cylinder switch is LOCK position.
  • There should be continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals when the door key cylinder switch is UNLOCK position.

 5-door:
  1. Remove the door panel (see page 20-144).
  2. Disconnect the 3P connector from the key cylinder switch.

Wire side of female terminals

  1. Check for continuity between the terminals.
  • There should be continuity between the No. 2 and No. 3 terminals when the door key cylinder switch is LOCK position.
  • There should be continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals when the door key cylinder switch is UNLOCK position.
