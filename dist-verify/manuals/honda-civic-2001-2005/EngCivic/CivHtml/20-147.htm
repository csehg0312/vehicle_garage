Front Door Outer Handle Replacement

 20-147  

  1. On driver's and some passenger's: Release the retainer clip (A), then remove the lock cylinder (B). Disconnect the cylinder rod (C).

  1. With cylinder switch: Remove the screw, then separate the lock cylinder (A) and cylinder switch (B).
    Fastener Location
    : Screw, 1

  1. With cylinder switch: Disconnect the cylinder switch connector (A) and detach the harness clips (B), then remove the cylinder switch (C).

  1. If equipped with a outer handle protector, disconnect the outer handle rod (A).
    Fastener Locations
    : Bolt, 2

  1. Remove the maintenance cap (B), and remove the bolts securing the outer handle (C). If equipped, release the hook (D), then remove the outer handle protector (E).
