Sunroof

Frame and Drain Tube Replacement

 20-199  

  1. Remove these items:
  • Headliner (see page 20-210)
  • Sunroof glass (see page 20-194)
  1. Put on gloves to protect your hands. Disconnect the motor connector (A), open/close-tilt/close switch connector (B) and the drain tubes (C), and remove the sunroof relays (D).
    Fastener Location
    : Bolt, 10

  1. Remove the interior harness (E) by detaching the harness clips (F).
  2. With an assistant holding the frame (G), remove the bolts, starting at the rear and release the rear hooks (H) by moving the frame forward, then remove the frame.
  3. With the help of an assistant, carefully remove the frame through the front door opening. Take care not to scratch the interior trim and body, or tear the seat covers.
