DTC Troubleshooting - with Side Airbags (cont'd)

 23-131  

  1. Disconnect the O1i connector (A) from the OPDS unit (B).

  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II) for 30 seconds then turn it off.
  2. Check the No. 9 (10A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.

Is the fuse OK?

YES - Short to ground in the OPDS unit; replace the OPDS unit.

NO - Short to ground in the No. 9 (10A) circuit.

  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 8 terminal of the O1i connector and body ground. There should be 0 - 1.0 ohm.

O1i CONNECTOR

Wire side of female terminals

Is the resistance as specified?

YES - Go to step 18.

NO - Open in the floor wire harness, OPDS unit harness, or poor ground (G551). If the G551 is OK, replace the faulty harness.

  1. Disconnect the battery negative cable and wait for 3 minutes.
  2. Disconnect the side airbag connectors (A).
