Sunroof

Drain Channel Slider and Cable Assembly Replacement

 20-59  

  1. Remove the frame (see page 20-57).
  1. Remove these parts from the frame:
  • Sunshade (see page 20-55)
  • Sunroof motor (see page 20-56)
  1. Put on gloves to protect your hands. Remove the slide stops (A), cable tube rear brackets (B), cable tube side bracket mounting bolts (C) and the cable tube mounting screws (D) from both sides of the frame (E).
    Fastener Locations
    C : Bolt, 2 D : Screw, 2 F : Screw, 2

G : Screw, 2

  1. While pushing down on the hook (A), slide the drain channel sliders (B) back on both sides.

  1. Turn both cable tube side brackets (A) up, and remove the drain channel sliders (B).
