DTC Troubleshooting - with Side Airbags (cont'd)

 23-132  

  1. Disconnect the U2o connector (A) from the SRS unit (B).

  1. Check resistance between the No. 3 terminal of the U2o connector and body ground. There should be 1 M ohm or more.

U2o CONNECTOR

Wire side of female terminals

Is the resistance as specified?

YES - Go to step 22.

NO - Short to ground in the floor wire harness or in the OPDS unit harness; replace the faulty harness.

  1. Check resistance between the No. 3 terminal of the U2o connector and the No. 7 terminal of the O1i connector. There should be 0 - 1.0 ohm.

U2o CONNECTOR

Wire side of female terminals

Is the resistance as specified?

YES - Go to step 23.

NO - Open in the floor wire harness or in the OPDS unit harness; replace the faulty harness.
