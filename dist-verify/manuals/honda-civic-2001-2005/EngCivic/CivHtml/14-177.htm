Transmission Housing

Bearing Removal and Installation

 14-177  

Special Tools Required
  • Snap ring pliers, 07LGC-0010100
  • Handle driver, 07949-3710001
  • Handle driver, 07749-0010000
  • Driver attachment, 62 x 68 mm, 07746-0010500
  • Driver attachment, 72 x 75 mm, 07746-0010600

NOTE: Coat all parts with ATF before assembly.

  1. To remove the mainshaft bearing (A), countershaft bearing (B) and sub-shaft bearing (C) from the transmission housing, expand each snap ring with the snap ring pliers, then push the bearing out.
    NOTE: Do not remove the snap ring unless it's necessary to clean the grooves in the housing.

  1. Install the bearings in the direction shown.
  1. Expand each snap ring with the snap ring pliers and install the bearing part-way into the housing.

  1. Release the pliers, then push the bearing down into the housing until the snap ring snaps in place around it.
  2. After installing the bearings verify that the snap rings (A) are seated in the bearing and housing grooves and that the ring end gaps (B) are correct.
