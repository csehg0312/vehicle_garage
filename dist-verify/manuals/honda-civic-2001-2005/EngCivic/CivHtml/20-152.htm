Doors

Rear Door Panel Removal/Installation

 20-152  

Special Tools Required

Trim pad remover, Snap-on # A 177 or equivalent, commercially available.

  1. Lower the glass fully.
  2. If applicable, remove the regulator handle (A) by pulling the clip (B) out with a wire hook (C).

  1. Remove the inner handle (A). Take care not to scratch the door panel.
  1. Pry out on the upper portion of the cover (B) to release the hooks (C and D), then remove the cover.
  2. Remove the screws.
  3. Pull out the inner handle forward and out half-way to release the hook (E).
  4. Disconnect the inner handle rod (F).
Fastener Locations
: Screw, 2

  1. Remove the grip cover (A).
  1. Using a flat-tip screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, insert a flat-tip screwdriver between the cover and door panel at the front edge, then pry out the cover.
  2. Pry out along the bottom to release the lower tabs (B).
  3. Pry out along the top to release the upper hooks (C), and rear hook (D).
Fastener Locations
B : Tab, 7 C, D : Hook, 7
