Transmission Housing/Flywheel Housing

ATF Pump Replacement

 14-358  

Special Tools Required

Snap ring pliers, 07LGC-0010100

  1. Remove the ATF pump drive sprocket bolts (A) and remove the ATF pump drive sprocket (B) and ATF pump drive chain (C).

  1. Move the pitot flange (D) toward its cutout (E) to clear the pitot pipe (F) under the pitot flange and remove the pitot flange.
  2. Expand the snap ring (A) under the ATF pump driven sprocket (B) with the special tool, then remove the ATF pump driven sprocket.

  1. Remove the old ATF pump body from the transmission housing.
  1. Install the 22 x 10 mm ATF pipe (A) and 18 x 10 mm pipe (B) in the transmission housing, then install the new O-rings (C) over the ATF pipes.

  1. Install the new ATF pump body (D). Do not pinch the O-rings.
  2. Install the snap ring (F) in the snap ring groove.
  3. Install the ATF pump driven sprocket.
  4. Install the pitot flange using its cutout to clear the pitot pipe.
  5. Install the ATF drive chain and ATF drive sprocket.
