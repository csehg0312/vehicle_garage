Window Defogger Rear

Window Antenna Coil Test

 22-163 Sub-window Antenna Coil Test

  1. Open the trunk lid.
  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (A) and 1P connector (B) from the window antenna coil (C).

  1. Check for continuity between the B2 terminal and body ground and between the A1 terminal and B1 terminal.
    There should be continuity.
    If there is no continuity, go to step 4.
  1. Remove the rear shelf (see page 20-65).
  2. Loosen the two bolts and remove the window antenna coil.
  1. Remove the right rear side trim panel (see page 20-67).
  1. Disconnect the 1P connectors (A) from the sub-window antenna coil (B).

  1. Check for continuity between the A1 and B1 terminals.
    There should be continuity.
    If there is no continuity, remove the two screws and replace the window antenna coil.
