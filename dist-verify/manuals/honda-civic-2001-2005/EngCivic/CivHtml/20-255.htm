Exterior Trim

Rear License Trim Replacement

 20-255  

NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove the tailgate lower trim panel (see page 20-209).
  2. On inside the tailgate, remove the bolts securing the rear license trim (A) and tailgate lock cylinder (B).
    Fastener Locations
    : Bolt, 2

  1. From inside the tailgate, release the clips (A, B) while holding the rear license trim (C), then remove the trim. Take care not to scratch the tailgate.

Fastener Locations
A : Clip, 2 B : Clip, 2

  1. Install the trim in the reverse order of removal and replace any damaged clips.
