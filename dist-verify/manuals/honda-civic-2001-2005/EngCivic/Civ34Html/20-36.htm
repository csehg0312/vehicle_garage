  1. Install the carpet in the reverse order of removal and note these items:
  • Take care not to damage, wrinkle or twist the carpet.
  • Make sure the seat harnesses are routed correctly.
  • Slip the carpet under the rear side trim panel on each side properly.
  • Replace the clip if it is damaged.
  • Reattach the cut areas under the heater around the parking brake lever with a wire tie.
  • When installing new carpet, cut the carpet (A) under the heater areas (B) and around the parking brake lever (C). After installing the new carpet, slip the carpet from the driver's and passenger's side through the space between the heater unit and body and reattach the cut area (C) around the parking brake lever with wire ties.
