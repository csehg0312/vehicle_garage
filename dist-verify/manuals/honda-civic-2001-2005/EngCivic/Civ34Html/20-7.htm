Door Outer Handle Replacement (cont'd)

 20-7  

  1. With cylinder switch: Disconnect the cylinder switch connector (A) and detach the harness clips (B), then remove the cylinder switch (C).

  1. On driver's and some passenger's: Release the cylinder rod protector (A) from the latch (B).
  1. Disconnect the outer handle rod (A).

Fastener Locations

: Bolt, 2

  1. Remove the maintenance cap (B) and remove the bolts securing the outer handle (C). Release the hook (D), then remove the outer handle protector (E) with the cylinder rod protector (F).
  2. Pull out the outer handle (A), then remove it.
