Consoles, Front and Rear Removal/Installation

 20-37  

NOTE: Take care not to scratch the front seat, dashboard and related parts.

  1. Remove the front console (A).
  1. Slide the front seat forward fully on each side.
  2. Remove the screws (B) from the rear portion of the rear console.
  3. Slide the front seat rearward fully on each side.
  4. Pull the front position of the rear console up to detach the clips (C) and hooks (D), then release the rear console from the front console (E).
  5. If equipped, disconnect the seat heater switch connector (F).
  6. Starting at the rear, pull the rear console up, then remove it.

Fastener Locations

B : Screw, 2 C : Clip, 2
  1. Remove the rear console (A).
  1. Remove the ashtray (B).
  2. Remove the console box mat (C).
  3. Remove the bolts (D), screws (E) and clips (F).
  4. Gently pull out the rear console to release the hooks (G) and pins (H).

Fastener Location

D : Bolt, 2 E : Screw, 2 F : Clip, 2

  1. Install the console in the reverse order of removal and note these items:
  • Replace any damaged clips.
  • Make sure the seat heater switch connector is plugged in properly (for some models).
