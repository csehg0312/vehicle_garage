How to Use This Manual


This manual covers the repairs of 2001 model series CIVIC that have been involved in an accident and it describes the work related to the replacement of damage body parts.
Please read through instructions and familiarise yourself with them before actually using this manual.
NOTE: Refer to the 2001 CIVIC Shop Manual (P/N. 60S5A00) for specifications, wire harness locations, safety stand support points, etc.
