General Safety Precautions

6-2  

  • Most paints contain substances that are harmful if inhaled or swallowed.
  • The following precautions are important items in order to maintain safe painting work.
  1. Wear an approved respirator and eye protection when painting.

  1. Wear approved gloves, appropriate clothing when painting. Avoid contact with skin.

  1. Spray paint only in a well ventilated area.
  1. Read the paint label before opening the container. Cover spilled paint with sand, or wipe it up at once.

  1. If paint gets in your mouth or your skin, rinse or wash thoroughly with water. If paint gets in your eyes, flush with water and get prompt medical attention.

  1. After the painting work is finished, wash face and gargle with water.

  1. Paint is flammable. Store it in a safe place, and keep it away from sparks, flames or cigarettes.
