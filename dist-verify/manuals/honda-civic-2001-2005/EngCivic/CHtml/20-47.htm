Trunk Lid Opener/Fuel Fill Door Opener Cable Replacement (cont'd)

 20-47  

  1. Remove the cushion tape (A) and detach the clip (B) with a clip remover. Remove the fuel fill door latch (C) by turning it 90° and detach the opener cable junction box (D) from the body.

    Fastener Locations

    A : Cushion tape, 1B and G : Clip, 2H : Clip, 3

  1. Disconnect the trunk lid opener cable (E) from the trunk lid latch (F), refer to the '01 CIVIC Shop Manual, P/N 62S5A00 on this CD (see page 20-130).
  2. Using a clip remover, detach the clip (G) from the body and detach the clips (H) from the trunk lid hinge.
  3. Remove the trunk lid opener/fuel fill door opener cable from the vehicle. Take care not to bend the cable.
  4. Install the opener cable in the reverse order of removal and note these items:
  • Align the mark on the cable with the clip on the bottom portion of the trunk lid hinge.
  • Replace any damaged clips.
