Engine Assembly

Engine Removal (cont'd)

 5-4  

  1. Remove the throttle cable (A) and cruise control cable (B) by loosening the locknuts (C), then slipping the cable ends out of the accelerator linkage. Take care not to bend the cables when removing them. Always replace any kinked cable with a new one.

  1. Remove the glove box stops, then open the glove box.
  2. Disconnect the Engine Control Module (ECM)/Powertrain Control Module (PCM) connectors.
  1. Remove the harness clamps (A) and grommet (B), then pull the engine wire harness through the bulkhead.

  1. Remove the adjusting plate mounting bolt (A), locknut (B) and mounting bolt (C), then remove the power steering (P/S) pump belt (D) and pump without disconnecting the P/S hoses.
