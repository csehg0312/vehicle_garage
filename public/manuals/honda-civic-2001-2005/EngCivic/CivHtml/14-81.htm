Automatic Transmission

DTC Troubleshooting (cont'd)

 14-81  

  1. Disconnect shift solenoid valves A and B connector (2P).
  1. Check for continuity between PCM connector terminals C6 and A23 or A24.

PCM CONNECTORS

Wire side of female terminals

Is there continuity?

YES - Repair short to ground in the wire between PCM connector terminal C6 and the shift solenoid valve A.

NO - Go to step 10.
  1. Measure the resistance between the No. 1 terminal of the shift solenoid valves A and B connector and body ground.

SHIFT SOLENOID VALVES A and B CONNECTORS (2P)

Terminal side of male terminals

Is there 12-25 ohm?

YES - Check for an open in the wire between PCM connector terminal C6 and the shift solenoid valve A.

NO - Replace the shift solenoid valves A and B.
