Sunroof

Wind Deflector Replacement

 20-53  

  1. Open the glass fully.
  1. Remove the screws (A, B) and release the hooks (C), then remove the wind deflector (D).
    Fastener Locations
    A : Screw, 2 B: Screw, 2

  1. Install the deflector (A) in the reverse order of removal, and check that the deflector arm base (B) and deflector link pivot base (C) line up with same alignment lines (D). If necessary, adjust them forward or backward so the edge of the deflector seal (E) touches the roof panel (F) evenly. Each base must be moved the same amount for adjustment.
