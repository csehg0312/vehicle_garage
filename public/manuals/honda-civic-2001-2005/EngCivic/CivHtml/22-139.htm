Interior Lights

Trunk Light Test/Replacement

 22-139 Trunk Lid Latch Switch Test

  1. Open the trunk lid.
  1. Carefully pry out the trunk light (A).
    Trunk Light: 5 W

  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (B) from the light.
  2. Check for continuity between the No. 1 (+) and No. 2 (–) terminals. There should be continuity.
    If there is no continuity, check the bulb. If the bulb is OK, replace the trunk light.
  1. Open the trunk lid.
  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (A) from the trunk lid latch (B).

  1. Check for continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals.
  • There should be continuity with the trunk lid open.
  • There should be no continuity with the trunk lid closed.
