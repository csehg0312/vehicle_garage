Clutch Master Cylinder Removal

 12-5a  

NOTE: Do not spill brake fluid on the vehicle; it may damage the paint; if brake fluid does contact the paint, wash it off immediately with water.

  1. Remove the brake fluid from the clutch master cylinder reservoir with a syringe.
  2. For the left-side steering vehicles with retaining clip holder (A) remove the retaining clip (B) after removing the retaining clip holder.

  1. Disconnect the clutch line (C) and reservoir hose (D). Plug the end of the clutch line and reservoir hose with a shop towel to prevent brake fluid from coming out.
  2. Pry out the lock pin (A) and pull the pedal pin (B) out from the yoke. Remove the nuts (C).
  1. Remove the clutch master cylinder.

  1. Remove the O-ring (A) and the clutch master cylinder seal (B) from the master cylinder (C).
