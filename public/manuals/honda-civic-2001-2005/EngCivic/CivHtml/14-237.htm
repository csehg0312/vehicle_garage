PCM Reset Procedures
  1. Write down the radio station presets.
  2. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  3. Remove NO.6 ECU (PCM) fuse (15 A) (A) from the under-hood fuse/relay box (B) for more than 10 seconds.

 How to End a Troubleshooting Session

This procedure must be done after any troubleshooting.

  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  2. Disconnect the special tool from the 16P Data Link Connector.
  3. Reset the PCM.
  4. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  5. Reset the radio preset stations and set the clock.
  6. To verify that the problem is repaired, test-drive the vehicle for several minutes at speeds over 30 mph (50 km/h).
