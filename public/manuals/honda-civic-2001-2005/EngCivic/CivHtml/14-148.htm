Automatic Transmission

Shift Cable Replacement (cont'd)

 14-148  

5-door Model
  1. Raise the vehicle and make sure it is securely supported (see page 1-15).
  2. Remove the ash tray, then remove the centre console lower cover.
  3. Remove the shift lever console panel.
  4. Shift the shift lever to position.
  5. Slide the lock tab (A) down, then pull the shift cable lock (B) at the joint in the shift cable end and shift cable end holder.

  1. Shift the shift lever to position, then separate the shift cable end (C) from the shift cable end holder (D).
  1. Rotate the socket holder (A) on the shift cable (B) counter-clockwise a quarter turn; the projection (C) on the socket holder faces direction to remove. Then slide the holder to remove the shift cable from the shift cable bracket base (D).

NOTE: Do not remove the shift cable by twisting the shift cable guide (E).

  1. Remove the shift cable guide bracket (A) and grommet (B), then remove the guide bracket from the cable.
