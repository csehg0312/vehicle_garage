Precautions and Procedures (cont'd)

 23-32  

  1. Disconnect the negative battery cable and wait at least 3 minutes.

Driver's Airbag

  1. Remove the access panel from the steering wheel, then disconnect the driver's airbag 2P connector (A) from the cable reel 2P connector (B).

Front Passenger's Airbag

  1. Remove the glove box, then disconnect the front passenger's airbag 2P connector (A) from the dashboard wire harness B 2P connector (B).

 Side Airbag
  1. Disconnect both side airbag 2P connectors (A) from the floor wire harness 2P connectors (B).

Seat Belt Tensioner

  1. Disconnect both seat belt tensioner 2P connectors (A) from the floor wire harness 2P connector (B).
