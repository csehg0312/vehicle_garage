Cylinder Head

Cylinder Head Cover Installation (cont'd)

 6-56  

  1. Tighten the bolts in 2 or 3 steps. In the final step, tighten all bolts, in sequence, to 9.8 Nm (1.0 kgf/m, 7.2 lbf/ft).

  1. Install the harness holder (A) and throttle cable clamps (B).
  1. Install the ignition coils (see page 4-24).
  2. Check that all tubes, hoses and connectors are installed correctly.
  3. After assembly, wait at least 30 minutes before filling the engine with oil.
