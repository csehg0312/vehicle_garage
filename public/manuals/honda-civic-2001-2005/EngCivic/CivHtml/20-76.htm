Driver's Dashboard Under Cover Removal/Installation

 20-76

Driver's Pocket Removal/Installation

NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • LHD is shown, RHD is symmetrical.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard under cover (A).
  1. Turn the lock knob (B) 45°.
  2. Gently pull down the rear edge to release the clip (C).
  3. Pull the cover away to release it from the clip (D).
Fastener Location
C : Clip, 2

  1. Install the cover in the reverse order of removal.

 NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  1. Remove the driver's pocket (A).
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard under cover.
  2. Open the pocket (LHD), and remove the screw (B).
  3. From behind the dashboard, push the pocket back by hand to release the clips (C).
  4. Release the hooks (D).
  5. If equipped, disconnect the cruise control main switch connector (E) or headlight adjuster switch connector (F), sunroof switch connector (G), and power mirror switch connector (H).

LHD:

Fastener Locations
B : Screw, 1 C : Clip, 2
