Seat Belts

Child Seat Tether Anchor Fitting Replacement

 23-12  

KQ model
  1. Open the tether anchor cap (A) on the middle of the rear shelf (B).

  1. Remove the anchor bolt (C), then remove the child seat tether anchor fitting (D). Do not remove the toothed washer (E) from the tether anchor fitting. Take care not to scratch the rear shelf.
  2. Install the tether anchor fitting in the reverse order of removal.
