Manual Transmission

Transmission Removal (cont'd)

 13-7  

  1. Raise vehicle and make sure it is securely supported.
  1. Drain transmission fluid with shop towel covering the front and rear beams to catch any spilled fluid (see page 13-3).
  2. Remove the splash shield.

  1. Remove exhaust pipe A (see step 28 on page 5-7).
  2. Remove the driveshafts (see page 16-3).
  3. Remove the three bolts securing the transmission rear mount.

  1. Remove the transmission rear mount (A) and the transmission rear mount bracket (B).

  1. Make reference marks (A) front suspension sub frame (B) and mounting bolts (C), then remove the front suspension sub frame.
