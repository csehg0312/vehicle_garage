Front Seat Removal/Installation (cont'd)

 20-88  

  1. For some models: Lift up the front seat, then detach the harness clip (A), and disconnect the seat belt switch connector (B) on the driver's seat, the seat heater connector (C) on the driver's and passenger's seats, the side airbag connector (D) on the driver's and passenger's seat, and the OPDS unit connector (E) on the passenger's seat. LHD is shown, RHD is symmetrical.

Driver's:

Passenger's:
  1. With the help of an assistant, carefully remove the front seat through the front door opening.
  1. Install the seat in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
  • Make sure each connector is plugged in properly.
  • If equipped with a side airbag, reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  • If equipped, enter the anti-theft code for the radio, then enter the customer's radio station presets.
