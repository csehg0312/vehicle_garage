Seats

Rear Seat Cushion Cover Replacement

 20-103  

  1. Remove the rear seat cushion, folding type rear seat (see page 20-95), fixed type rear seat (see page 20-96).
  1. Release all the clips (A) from under the seat cushion, and fold back the seat cushion cover (B).

  1. Pull back the edge of the seat cushion cover all the way around, and release the clips (A), then remove the seat cushion cover.
  1. Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
  • To prevent wrinkles, make sure the material is stretched evenly over the pad before securing the clips.
  • Replace any clips (A) you removed with new ones. Install them with commercially available upholstery ring pliers (B).
