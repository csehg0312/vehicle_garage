Cruise Control

Actuator/Cable Replacement

 4-48  

  1. Loosen the locknut (A), then disconnect the actuator cable (B) from the throttle linkage.

  1. Disconnect the 4P connector (A) from the actuator.

  1. Remove the two mounting bolts, and remove the actuator assembly (B).
  1. Remove the actuator cover.

  1. Remove the actuator cable from the actuator.

  1. Install the reverse order of removal, and adjust the free play at the throttle linkage after connecting the actuator cable.
