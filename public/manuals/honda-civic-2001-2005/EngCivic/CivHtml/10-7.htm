Cooling System

Water Pump Inspection

 10-7 Water Pump Replacement

  1. Remove the timing belt (see page 6-18).
  1. Turn the water pump pulley counter-clockwise. Check that it turns freely.
  2. Check for signs of seal leakage. A small amount of ''weeping'' from the bleed hole (A) is normal.
  1. Remove the timing belt (see page 6-18).
  1. Remove the water pump (A) by removing four bolts.

  1. Inspect and clean the O-ring groove and mating surface with the cylinder block.
  2. Install the water pump with a new O-ring (B) in the reverse order of removal.
  3. Clean up any spilled engine coolant.
