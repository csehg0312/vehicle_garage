Front and Rear Door Glass Adjustment

 20-162  

NOTE: Check the weatherstrips and glass run channel for damage or deterioration, and replace them if necessary.
  1. Place the vehicle on a firm, level surface.
  2. Remove these items:
  • Door panel, front door (see page 20-144), rear door (see page 20-152)
  • Plastic cover, front door (see page 20-140), rear door (see page 20-142)
  1. Carefully move the glass (A) until you can see the glass mounting bolts (B), then loosen them.

 Rear:

  1. Push the glass (A) against the channel (C), then tighten the glass mounting bolts.
  2. Check that the glass moves smoothly.
  3. Raise the glass fully and check for gaps. Also check that the glass (A) contacts the glass run channel (B) evenly.
