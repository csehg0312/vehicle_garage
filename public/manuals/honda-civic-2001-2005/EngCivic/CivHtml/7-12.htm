Engine Block

Crankshaft and Piston Removal (cont'd)

 7-12  

  1. Remove the upper bearing halves from the connecting rods, and set them aside with their respective caps.
  2. Reinstall the bearing cap bridge and bearings on the engine in the proper order.
  3. If you can feel a ridge of metal or hard carbon around the top of each cylinder, remove it with a ridge reamer (A). Follow the reamer manufacturer's instructions. If the ridge is not removed, it may damage the pistons as they are pushed out.

  1. Use the wooden handle of a hammer (A) to drive out the pistons (B).

  1. Reinstall the connecting rod bearing and caps after removing each piston/connecting rod assembly.
  1. To avoid mix-up on reassembly, mark each piston/connecting rod assembly with its cylinder number.

NOTE: The existing number on the connecting rod does not indicate its position in the engine, it indicates the rod size.
