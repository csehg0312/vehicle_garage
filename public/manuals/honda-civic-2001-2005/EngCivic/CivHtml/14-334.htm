Honda Multi Matic Transmission/CVT

Transmission Installation (cont'd)

 14-334  

  1. Attach the flywheel to the drive plate (A) with eight bolts (B). Rotate the crankshaft pulley as necessary to tighten the bolts to 1/2 of the specified torque, then to the final torque, in a criss-cross pattern. After installing the last bolt, check that the crankshaft rotates freely.

  1. Install the flywheel cover (C) and engine stiffeners (D).
  2. Connect the ball joints (A) to the lower arms (B) and install the castle nuts (C) and spring clips (D).

  1. Connect the ball joint pins (E) to the lower arms and install the nuts (F). Insert a 5 mm Allen wrench (G) in the top of the ball joint pins and tighten the nuts.
  1. Install the exhaust pipe A.

  1. Connect the secondary HO2S connector (A) and install the harness (B) on its clamps (C).
