Dashboard Centre Lower Cover Removal/Installation

RHD:

Fastener Locations
B : Screw, 1 C: Clip, 2

  1. Install the pocket in the reverse order of removal, and make sure each connector is plugged in properly (for some models).

 NOTE:
  • When prying with a flat-tip screwdriver, wrap it with protective tape, and apply protective tape around the related parts, to prevent damage.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  1. Remove the dashboard centre lower cover (A).
  1. Remove the ashtray (B), and remove the screw (C).
  2. Gently pull out the cover to release the clips (D, E).
  3. Disconnect the cigarette lighter connector (F), cigarette light connector (G) and ashtray light bulb socket (H).
Fastener Locations
C : Screw, 1 D : Clip, 5 E : Clip,1

  1. Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and make sure each connector is plugged in properly and bulb socket is connected properly.
