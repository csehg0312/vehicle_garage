Exterior Trim

Rear License Trim Replacement

 20-118  

  1. On the inside the trunk lid, remove the nut (A), and release the clips (B), then remove the rear license trim (C). Take care not to scratch the trunk lid.

Fastener Locations
A : Nut, 1 B : Clip, 4

  1. Install the trim in the reverse order of removal, and replace any damaged clips.
