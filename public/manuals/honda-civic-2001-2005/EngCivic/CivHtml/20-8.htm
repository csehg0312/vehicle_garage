Front Door Panel Removal/Installation (cont'd)

 20-8

Front door Outer Handle Replacement

  1. Release the clips that hold the door panel (A) with a commercially available trim pad remover (B), then remove the door panel by pulling it upward. Passenger's door: Disconnect the power window switch connector (for some models). Remove the door panel with as little bending as possible to avoid creasing or breaking it.

    Fastener Locations
    : Clip, 7

  1. Install the door panel in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
  • Replace any damaged clips.
  • Make sure the connectors are plugged in properly, and the rod is connected properly.
  • If applicable, install the regulator handle so it points forward and up at a 45° angle with the glass fully closed.

 NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Raise the glass fully.
  2. Remove these items:
  • Door panel (see page 20-7)
  • Plastic cover, as necessary (see page 20-3)
  1. Release the retainer clip (A), then remove the lock cylinder (B). Disconnect the cylinder rod (C).

  1. With cylinder switch: Remove the screw, then separate the lock cylinder (A) and cylinder switch (B).
    Fastener Location
    : Screw, 1
