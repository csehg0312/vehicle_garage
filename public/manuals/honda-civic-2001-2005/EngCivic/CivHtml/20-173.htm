Mirrors

Rearview Mirror Replacement

 20-173  

  1. Slide the rearview mirror (A) down toward the bottom of the windshield, then detach it from the spring (B) in the mount (C) and remove the rearview mirror. Take care not to scratch the mirror base (D).

  1. If necessary, remove the spring from the mount.
  2. Fit the mirror base over the mount.
  1. Secure the mirror by turning the base 90°.
