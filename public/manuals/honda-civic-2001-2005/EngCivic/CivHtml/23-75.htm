DTC 3-5: Short to Ground in Left Side Seat Belt Tensioner
  1. Erase the DTC memory (see page 23-38).
  2. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and check that the SRS indicator light comes on for about 6 seconds and then goes off.

Does the SRS indicator light stay on?

YES - Go to step 3.

NO - Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures (see page 23-38).

  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF. Disconnect the battery negative cable and wait for 3 minutes.
  2. Disconnect the TL1o connector from the TL1i connector (A).

  1. Connect the special tool (2 ohm) to the TL1i connector.
  1. Reconnect the battery negative cable.
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  2. Read the DTC.

Is DTC 3-5 indicated?

YES - Go to step 9.

NO - Short to ground in the left side seat belt tensioner; replace the left side seat belt for 4-door model (see page 23-5) or 5-door model (see page 23-15).

  1. Disconnect the battery negative cable and wait for 3 minutes.
  2. Disconnect the T2o connector (A) from the T2i connector (B).
    4-door 5-door

  1. Connect the special tool (2 ohm) to the T2i connector.
  2. Reconnect the battery negative cable.
  3. Erase the DTC memory.
  4. Read the DTC.

Is DTC 3-5 indicated?

YES - Go to step 15.

NO - Short to ground in the floor wire harness; replace the floor wire harness.

  1. Disconnect the battery negative cable and wait for 3 minutes.
