Antenna Mast Replacement

 22-215 Roof Antenna Replacement

  1. Disconnect the sub antenna lead from the antenna lead.
  1. Carefully pull the mast antenna out.
  1. Remove the rear part of headliner (see page 20-210).
  1. Disconnect the antenna lead connector (A) and 1P connector (B) from the roof antenna (C).

  1. Remove the mounting nut and the antenna.
