Honda Multi Matic Transmission/CVT

Transmission Removal (cont'd)

 14-328  

  1. Make the appropriate reference lines at positions A and B that line up with the centre of the sub-frame mounting bolts (C).

  1. Support the sub-frame with a 4 x 4 x 40 in. piece of wood and a jack.
  1. Remove the 10 x 1.25 mm bolts (A) and four sub-frame mounting bolts (B), then lower the sub-frame (C).

  1. Remove the driveshafts from the differential. Coat all precision finished surfaces with clean engine oil, then tie plastic bags over the driveshaft ends.
