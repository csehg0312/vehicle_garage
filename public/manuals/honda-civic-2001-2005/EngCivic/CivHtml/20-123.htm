Fuel Pipe Protector Replacement

 20-123

Rear Air Outlet Replacement

  1. Remove the clips, then remove the fuel pipe protector (A). Take care not to scratch the body.

Fastener Locations
: Clip, 3

  1. Install the protector in the reverse order of removal, and replace any damaged clips.
  1. Remove the rear bumper (see page 20-105).
  1. Detach the hooks (A), then remove the rear air outlet (B). Take care not to scratch the body.

  1. Install the air outlet by pushing on the hook portions until the hooks snap into place.
