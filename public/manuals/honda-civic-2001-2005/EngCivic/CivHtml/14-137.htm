Automatic Transmission

Shift Lever Removal

 14-137  

4-door Model
  1. Shift the transmission into position.
  2. Remove the centre console panel and centre console (see page 20-73).
  3. Remove the nut securing the shift cable end (A), then separate the cable end from the shift lever assembly.
  1. Rotate the socket holder (A) on the shift cable (B) toward the shift lever a quarter turn, then slide the holder out to remove the shift cable from the shift cable bracket base (C).

NOTE: Do not remove the shift cable by twisting the shift cable guide (D).

  1. Disconnect the A/T gear position indicator panel light/park pin switch connector (4P) (A) and shift lock solenoid connector (2P) (B).

  1. Remove the four bolts, then remove the shift lever assembly.
