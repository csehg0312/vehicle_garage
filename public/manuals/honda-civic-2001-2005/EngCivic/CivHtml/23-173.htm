Cable Reel Replacement

 23-173  

Removal
  1. Make sure the wheels are aligned straight ahead.
  2. Disconnect the battery negative cable and wait at least 3 minutes.
  3. Remove the driver's airbag (see page 23-165).
  4. Disconnect the connector (A) from the cruise control set/resume switch, then remove the steering wheel bolt (B).

  1. Align the front wheels straight ahead, then remove the steering wheel with a steering wheel puller (see page 17-6).
    Do not tap on the steering wheel or steering column shaft when removing the steering wheel.

  1. Remove the dashboard lower cover (A).

  1. Remove the column cover screws (A), then remove the column covers (B, C).
