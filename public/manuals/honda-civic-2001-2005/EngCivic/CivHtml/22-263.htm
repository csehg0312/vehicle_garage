Security System

Door Lock Knob Switch Test (cont'd)

 22-263  

Driver's door (5-door):
  1. Remove the driver's door panel (see page 20-144).
  2. Disconnect the 6P connector (A) from the super locking actuator.

Terminal side of male terminals

  1. Check for continuity between the terminals.
  • There should be continuity between the No. 3 and No. 4 terminals when the door lock knob switch is LOCK position.
  • There should be continuity between the No. 1 and No. 4 terminals when the door lock knob switch is UNLOCK position.

 Passenger's door (5-door):
  1. Remove the passenger's door panel (see page 20-7).
  2. Disconnect the 3P connector (A) from the actuator.

Terminal side of male terminals

  1. Check for continuity between the terminals.
  • There should be continuity between the No. 1 and No. 4 terminals when the door lock knob switch is UNLOCK position.
