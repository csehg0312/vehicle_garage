Shafts and Clutches

Countershaft Reverse Selector Hub Installation

 14-197  

Special Tools Required

Driver 40 mm I.D., 07746-0030100

  1. Wrap the countershaft splines with tape to prevent O-ring damage.
  2. Install the new O-rings (A), then remove the tape on the shaft splines.

  1. Install the 3rd clutch (B), splined washer (C), thrust needle bearing (D), 3rd gear collar (E), needle bearing (F), 3rd gear (G), thrust needle bearing (H), 2nd gear (I), 28 mm distance collar (J), needle bearing (K) and 4th gear (L) on the countershaft (M).
  1. Slide the reverse selector hub (A) over the countershaft (B), then press it into place with the special tool and a press.
