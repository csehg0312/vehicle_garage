Doors

Front Door Outer Handle Replacement

 20-146  

NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Raise the glass fully.
  2. Remove these items:
  • Door panel (see page 20-144)
  • Plastic cover, as necessary (see page 20-140)
  1. If equipped, remove the screw, pull out the lock rod protector (A) to release the hook (B) from the latch (C), then remove it.
Fastener Location
: Screw, 1

  1. Pull the glass run channel (A) away as necessary and remove the bolts, then remove the centre lower channel (B) by pulling it downward.

Fastener Location
: Bolt, 1

  1. If equipped, pull out the bottom of the cylinder rod protector (A) to release the hook (B) from the latch (C) and release the upper hook (D) from the outer handle protector (E), then remove the protector.
