Sunshade Replacement

 20-55  

  1. Remove the drain channel (see page 20-54).
  1. Slide the sunshade (A) until you can see both sunshade slider spacers (B).
    Fastener Locations
    : Screw, 4

  1. Remove the screws, then remove both spacers.
  1. While lifting the front portion of the sunshade (A), move the sunshade forward until you can see both sunshade rear hooks (B). Do not damage the sunshade and hooks.

    Fastener Locations
    : Screw, 4

  1. Remove the screws, then remove both hooks.
