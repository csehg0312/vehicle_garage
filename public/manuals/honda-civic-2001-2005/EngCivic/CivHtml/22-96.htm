Vehicle Speed Signal Circuit Troubleshooting (cont'd)

 22-96 VSS Replacement

CVT

Before testing, check the No. 9 (10A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box and No. 10 (7.5A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.

  1. Start the engine and check the Malfunction Indicator Lamp (MIL).

Does the MIL come on?

YES – Troubleshoot the DTC (see page 11-90) and recheck.

NO – Go to step 2.

  1. Check the multiplex control unit DTC (see page 22-273).

Is the DTC indicated?

YES – Troubleshoot the DTC (see page 22-273) and recheck.

NO – Inspect the connector and socket terminals of the gauge assembly connectors. If the terminals look OK, replace the gauge assembly.

  1. Remove the intake resonator.
  1. Disconnect the 3P connector (A) from the vehicle speed sensor (VSS) (B).

  1. Remove the mounting bolt, then remove the VSS.
  2. Install the VSS in the reverse order of removal.
