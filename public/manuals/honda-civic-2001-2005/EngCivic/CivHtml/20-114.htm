Exterior Trim

Cowl Covers Replacement

 20-114  

NOTE: LHD is shown, RHD is symmetrical.

  1. Remove the windshield wiper arms (see page 22-179.
  2. Using a clip remover, detach the clips (A), then remove the hood rear seal (B), and detach the clips (C) from the cowl covers. Take care not to scratch the cowl covers.
    Fastener Locations
    A : Clip, 9 C : Clip, 3 D : Clip, 5 E : Clip, 3

  1. Detach the clips (D, E) and release the hook (F) by carefully pulling the passenger's cowl cover (G) upward, and pull the cover forward to release the hooks (H), then remove the cover. Take care not to scratch the body.
  2. Detach the clips (D, E) by carefully pulling the driver's cowl cover (I) upward, and pull the cover forward to release the hooks (J), then remove the cover. Take care not to scratch the body.
  3. Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and replace any damaged clips.
