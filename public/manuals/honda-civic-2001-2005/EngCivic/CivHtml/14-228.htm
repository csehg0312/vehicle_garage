A/T Differential

Final Driven Gear Replacement

 14-228

Carrier Bearing Replacement

  1. Remove the final driven gear from the differential carrier. The final driven gear bolts have left-hand threads.

  1. Install the final driven gear with the chamfered side on the inner bore (A) facing the differential carrier.
  2. Tighten the bolts to 103 Nm (10.5 kgf/m, 75.9 lbf/ft) in a criss-cross pattern.

 Special Tools Required

Driver 40 mm I.D., 07746-0030100

  1. Remove the bearings (A) with a commercially available bearing puller (B) and stepper adapter (C).

  1. Install the new bearings with the special tool and a press. Press the bearing on until it bottoms.
