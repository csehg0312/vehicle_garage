  • Handle driver, 07749-0010000
  • Driver attachment, 72 x 75 mm, 07746-0010600
  1. Remove the secondary gear shaft bearing (A) from the transmission housing (B) with the special tool.

  1. Install the new secondary gear shaft bearing until it bottoms in the transmission housing with the special tools.
