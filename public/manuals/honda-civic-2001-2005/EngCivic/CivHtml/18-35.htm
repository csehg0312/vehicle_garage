Rear Suspension

Trailing Arm Replacement

 18-35  

  1. Raise the rear of the vehicle and make sure it is securely supported. Remove the rear wheels.
  1. Remove the knuckle (see page 18-26).
  2. Place the floor jack under the trailing arm (A) to support it.

  1. Remove the flange nut (B) and disconnect the stabiliser link (C) from the trailing arm.
  2. Remove the flange bolt (D) and disconnect the damper (E) from the trailing arm.
  3. Remove the trailing arm front mounting bolts (A).
  1. Remove the trailing arm rear mounting bolt (A).

  1. Lower the jack and remove the trailing arm.
  2. Install the trailing arm in the reverse order of removal and note these items:
  • First install all the suspension components and lightly tighten bolts and nuts, then place a jack under the trailing arm and raise the suspension to load it with the vehicle's weight before fully tightening bolts and nuts to the specified torque values.
  • Tighten all the mounting hardware to the specified torque values.
  • Before installing the wheel, clean the mating surface of the brake drum or disc and the inside of the wheel.
  • Check the wheel alignment and adjust it if necessary (see page 18-4).
