Front Door Glass and Regulator Replacement

 20-11  

NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove these items:
  • Door panel (see page 20-7)
  • Plastic cover, as necessary (see page 20-3)
  1. Carefully raise the glass (A) until you can see the bolts, then remove them. Carefully pull the glass out through the window slot. Take care not to drop the glass inside the door.
    Fastener Locations
    : Bolt, 2

  1. Power window: Disconnect and detach the connector (A) and harness clip (B) from the door.

    Fastener Locations
    C : Bolt, 2 D : Bolt, 4

  1. Remove the bolts (C), and loosen the bolts (D), then remove the regulator (F) through the hole in the door. Power window is shown, manual window is similar.
  2. Grease all the sliding surfaces of the regulator (A) where shown.
