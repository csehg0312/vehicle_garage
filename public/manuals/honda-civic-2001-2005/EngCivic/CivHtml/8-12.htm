Engine Lubrication

Oil/Air Separator Installation

 8-12 Oil Pressure Switch Replacement

  1. Clean and dry the oil/air separator mating surfaces.
  1. Apply liquid gasket, Part No 08C70-K0234M, 08C70-K0334M or 08C70-X0331S, evenly to the block mating surface of the oil/air separator and to the inner threads of the bolt holes.

  1. Install the oil/air separator.
  1. Disconnect the oil pressure switch connector, then remove the oil pressure switch.

  1. Apply liquid gasket to the oil pressure switch threads, then install the oil pressure switch.
