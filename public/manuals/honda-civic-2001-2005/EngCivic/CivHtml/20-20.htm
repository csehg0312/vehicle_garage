Rear Door Glass, Quarter Glass and Regulator Replacement (cont'd)

  1. Remove the quarter glass (A). Take care not to damage the outer weatherstrip (B).

  1. Carefully remove the glass (A) out through the window slot. Take care not to drop the glass inside the door.
  1. Power window: Disconnect and detach the connector (A) from the door.

    Fastener Locations
    B : Bolt, 2 C : Bolt, 4

  1. Remove the bolts (B), and loosen the bolts (C), then remove the regulator (D) through the hole in the door. Power window is shown, manual window is similar.
  2. Grease all the sliding surfaces, Japan-produced shape (A), except Japan-produced shape (B) of the regulator (C) where shown.
