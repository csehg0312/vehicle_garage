Interior Lights

Glove Box Light Test/Replacement

 22-141  

5-door:
  1. Remove the glove box (see page 20-220).
  2. Disconnect the 2P connector (A) from the glove box light (B).
    GLOVE BOX LIGHT: 3.4 W

  1. Check for continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals.
  • If there is no continuity, check the bulb. If the bulb is OK, replace the glove box light.
