Hood Seal Replacement

  1. Check that the hood opens properly and locks securely.
  1. Apply touch-up paint to the hinge mounting bolts and around the hinges.
  2. Grease each location of the hood latch and hood hinge as indicated by the arrows.
  1. Using a clip remover, detach the clips, then remove the hood seal (A). Take care not to scratch the hood.

Fastener Locations
: Clip, 20 or 12

European model:

Except european model:

  1. Install the seal in the reverse order of removal and replace any damaged clips.
