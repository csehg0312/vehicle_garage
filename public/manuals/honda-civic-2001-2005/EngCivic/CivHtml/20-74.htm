Centre Console Rear Cover Replacement

 20-74

Centre Console Trim Replacement

NOTE: Take care not to scratch the centre console.
  1. Remove the centre console (see page 20-73).
  2. Remove the screws, then remove the centre console rear cover (A).
    Fastener Locations
    : Screw, 2

  1. Install the cover in the reverse order of removal.

 NOTE: Take care not to scratch the centre console.
  1. Remove the centre console (see page 20-73).
  2. Remove the screws, then remove the centre console trim (A). MT model is shown, AT model is similar.
    Fastener Locations
    : Screw, 4

  1. Install the trim in the reverse order of removal.
