PGM-FI System

Injector Replacement

 11-113  

  1. Relieve fuel pressure (see page 11-130).
  2. Remove the air cleaner (see page 11-146).
  3. Disconnect the connectors from the injectors (A), the manifold absolute pressure (MAP) sensor (B), the evaporative emission (EVAP) canister purge valve (C), and the throttle position (TP) sensor (D).

  1. Disconnect the quick-connect fittings (E).
  2. Disconnect the hoses from the fuel rail (F).
  3. Remove the fuel rail mounting nuts (G) from the fuel rail.
  4. Remove the injector clip (H) from the injector.
  5. Remove the injector from the fuel rail.
