Front and Rear Door Glass Adjustment

 20-25  

Rear:

  1. Push the glass (A) against the channel (C), then tighten the glass mounting bolts.
  2. Check that the glass moves smoothly.
  3. Raise the glass fully, and check for gaps. Also check that the glass (A) contacts the glass run channel (B) evenly.
  1. Check for water leaks. Run water over the roof and on the sealing area as shown, and note these items:
  • Use a 12 mm (1/2 in.) diameter hose (A).
  • Adjust the rate of water flow as shown (B).
  • Do not use a nozzle.
  • Hold the hose about 300 mm (12 in.) away from the door (C).

  1. Attach the plastic cover, and install the door panel, front door (see page 20-7), rear door (see page 20-15).
