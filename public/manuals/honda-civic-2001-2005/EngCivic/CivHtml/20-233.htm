Front Seat Armrest Replacement

 20-233  

For some models:

NOTE:

  • When prying with a flat-tip screwdriver, wrap it with protective tape to prevent damage.
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  1. Release the tabs (A), then remove the front cover (B).
    Remove the clip, then remove the rear cover (C) from the armrest frame (D).
Fastener Location
: Clip, 1

  1. Remove the nut, the spring washer (A) and the washer (B), then remove the armrest (C). If necessary, remove the bushings (D) from the armrest.

Fastener Location
: Nut, 1

  1. Remove the bolts, then remove the armrest pin (A).
    Fastener Locations
    : Bolt, 2

  1. Install the armrest in the reverse order of removal and replace the clip if it's damaged.
