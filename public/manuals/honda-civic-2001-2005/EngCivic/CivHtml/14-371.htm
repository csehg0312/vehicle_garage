Clutch Reassembly (cont'd)

  1. Compress the return spring.

  1. Install the snap ring (A).
  1. Remove the special tools.
  1. Install the disc spring in the direction shown.

  1. Soak the clutch discs thoroughly in ATF for a minimum of 30 minutes. Before installing the plates and discs, make sure the inside of the clutch drum is free of dirt and other foreigNmatter.
  2. Starting with a clutch plate, alternately install the clutch plates and discs. Install the clutch end plate (A) with the flat side toward the disc (B).
