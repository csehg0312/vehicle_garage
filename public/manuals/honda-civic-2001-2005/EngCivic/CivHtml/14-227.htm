A/T Differential

Differential Carrier Replacement

 14-227  

  1. Remove the snap ring (A) from the differential assembly (B).

  1. Remove the 5 x 10 mm roller (A) and the speedometer drive gear (B).

  1. Remove final driven gear bolts, then separate the final driven gear and the differential carrier. The final driven gear bolts have left-hand threads.
  1. Install the final driven gear (A) on the new differential carrier (B) with the chamfered side on the inner bore facing the differential carrier.

  1. Tighten the bolts to 103 Nm (10.5 kgf/m, 75.9 lbf/ft) in a criss-cross pattern.
  2. Install the speedometer drive gear (A) with its chamfered side facing the carrier. Align the cutout on the bore of the speedometer drive gear with the 5 x 10 mm roller hole, then install the 5 x 10 mm roller.

  1. Align the hooked end (B) of the snap ring (C) with the spring pin (D) in the pinion shaft (E), then install the snap ring in the differential carrier groove.
