Steering Gearbox Overhaul (cont'd)

 17-41  

  1. Remove the stop washer (A), the 12 mm flange bolts (B), O-rings (C), and bracket (D) from the steering gearbox.

  1. Remove the boot guard (A) by removing the 8 mm flange bolt (C) on the rack end.

  1. Remove the boot band (D) and clip (E). Pull the boot B and away from the end of the steering gearbox. Remove the rack end plug (F).
  1. Loosen the locknut (A), then remove the rack guide screw (B), spring (C) and rack guide (D) from the steering gearbox.

  1. Remove the snap ring (A) and backup ring (B) from the cylinder of the steering gearbox.
