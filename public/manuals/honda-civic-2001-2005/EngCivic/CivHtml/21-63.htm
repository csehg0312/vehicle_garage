Air Mix Control Cable Adjustment

 21-63 Mode Control Cable Adjustment

Except 4-door, 5-door KE Model
  1. From under the hood, open the cable clamp (A), then disconnect the heater valve cable (B) from the heater valve arm (C).

  1. From under the dash, disconnect the air mix control cable housing from the cable clamp (A) and disconnect the air mix control cable (B) from the air mix control linkage (C).

  1. Set the temperature control dial to MAX COOL.
  2. Turn the air mix control linkage fully counter-clockwise as shown above and hold it. Attach the air mix control cable to the air mix control linkage, then snap the air mix control cable housing into the cable clamp.
  3. From under the hood, turn the heater valve arm to the fully closed position as shown above and hold it. Attach the heater valve cable to the heater valve arm and gently pull on the heater valve cable housing to take up any slack, then install the heater valve cable housing into the cable clamp.

 Except 4-door, 5-door KE Model
  1. From under the dash, disconnect the mode control cable housing from the cable clamp (A) and disconnect the mode control cable (B) from the mode control linkage (C).

  1. Set the mode control dial to VENT.
  2. Turn the mode control linkage fully counter-clockwise as shown above and hold it. Attach the mode control cable to the mode control linkage, then snap the mode control cable housing into the cable clamp.
