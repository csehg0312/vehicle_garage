Dashboard

Glove Box Removal/Installation

 20-79  

SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations (see page 23-23, and the precautions and procedures (see page 23-25) in the SRS section before performing repairs or service.

NOTE: Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.

LHD

  1. Remove the passenger's dashboard lower cover.
  2. While holding the glove box (A), remove the glove box stop (B) on each side.

  1. Remove the bolts, then remove the glove box.
    Fastener Locations
    : Bolt, 2

  1. Install the glove box in the reverse order of removal.

 RHD
  1. Remove the passenger's dashboard lower cover.
  2. Remove the bolts securing the glove box (A).
    Fastener Locations
    : Bolt, 2

  1. While holding the glove box, release the glove box stop (B) on each side from the dashboard by pushing it inside, then remove the glove box.
  2. Install the glove box in the reverse order of removal.
