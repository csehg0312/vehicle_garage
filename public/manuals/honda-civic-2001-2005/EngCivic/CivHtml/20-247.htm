Hood

Hood Insulator Replacement

 20-247  

For Some Models
  1. Disconnect the windshield washer tubes (A).
    Fastener Locations
    : Clip, 10

  1. Using a clip remover, detach the clips. Release the hooks (B), then remove the hood insulator (C). Take care not to scratch the hood.
  2. Install the insulator in the reverse order of removal and replace any damaged clips.
