Rear Air Outlet Replacement

 20-262  

  1. Remove the rear bumper (see page 20-244).
  1. Detach the hooks (A), then remove the rear air outlet (B). Take care not to scratch the body.
    Fastener Locations
    : Screw, 2

  1. Install the air outlet by pushing on the hook portions until the hooks snap into place.
