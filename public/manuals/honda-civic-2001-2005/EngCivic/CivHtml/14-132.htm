Automatic Transmission

Transmission Installation (cont'd)

 14-132  

  1. Install the rear mount bracket bolts.

  1. Remove the transmission jack from the transmission.
  2. Attach the torque converter to the drive plate (A) with eight bolts (B). Rotate the crankshaft pulley as necessary to tighten the bolts to 1/2 of the specified torque, then to the final torque, in a criss-cross pattern. After installing the last bolt, check that the crankshaft rotates freely.

  1. Install the torque converter cover (C) and engine stiffeners (D).
  1. Tighten the crankshaft pulley bolt as necessary (see page 6-16).
  1. Install the control lever (A) with the shift cable (B) on the control shaft (C). Do not bend the shift cable excessively.

  1. Secure the shift cable holder (D) with the bolts on the transmission.
  2. Install the lock bolt (E) with a new lock washer (F), then bend the lock washer tab against the bolt head.
  3. Install the shift cable covers (G).
