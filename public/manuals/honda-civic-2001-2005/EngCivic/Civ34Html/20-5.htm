Doors

Door panel removal/Installation (cont'd)

 20-5  

  1. Pull out the switch panel (A) to release the hook (B), then remove the panel from the door panel. Disconnect the power window switch connector (C) (except manual window).

  1. Release the clips that hold the door panel (A) with a commercially available trim pad remover (B), then remove the door panel by pulling it upward. Remove the door panel with as little bending as possible to avoid creasing or breaking it.

Fastener Locations

: Clip, 9
  1. Disconnect the inner handle cable (A) from the inner handle (B) and if equipped, disconnect the power door lock switch connector (C). Take care not to bend the cable.

  1. Detach the harness clip (A) from the door panel (B).

  1. Install the door panel in the reverse order of removal and note these items:
  • Replace any damaged clips.
  • Make sure the cable is connected securely and the connector is plugged in properly.
  • Install the regulator handle so it points forward with the glass fully closed.
