Exterior Lights

Taillight Replacement

 22-26  

Taillights:
  1. Open the trunk lid.
  2. Disconnect each connector from the taillight.

Brake/Taillight:21/5 W

Back-up Light18 W

Turn Signal Light:27 W

Side Marker Light

(KK, KX, KY):3.8 W

  1. Remove the four nuts and taillight.
  2. Install in the reverse order of removal.
  3. When installing the taillight check the gasket; if it is distorted or stays compressed, replace it.
  4. After installing the taillight run water over the taillight to make sure it does not leak.
