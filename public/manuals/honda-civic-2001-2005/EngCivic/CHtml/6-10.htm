Cylinder Head

Valve Clearance Adjustment (cont'd)

 6-10  

  1. Rotate the crankshaft 180° counter-clockwise (camshaft pulley turns 90°). The ''UP'' mark (A) on the camshaft pulley should be toward the exhaust side of the head.

  1. Check and if necessary, adjust the valve clearance on No. 3 cylinder.
  2. Rotate the crankshaft 180° counter-clockwise to bring No. 4 piston to TDC. TDC marks (A) are visible again.

  1. Check and if necessary, adjust the valve clearance on No. 4 cylinder.
  1. Rotate the crankshaft 180° counter-clockwise to bring No. 2 piston to TDC. The ''UP'' mark (A) should be on the intake side of the head.

  1. Check and if necessary, adjust the valve clearance on No. 2 cylinder.
  2. Install the cylinder head cover, refer to the '01 Civic Shop Manual on this CD on this CD (see page 6-55).
