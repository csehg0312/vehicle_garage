Cylinder Head Installation

  1. Set the rocker arm assembly in place and loosely install the bolts. Make sure that the rocker arms are properly positioned on the valve stems.
  1. Tighten each bolt 2 turns at a time, in the sequence shown below to ensure that the rockers do not bind on the valves.

Specified torque:

8 mm bolts:20 Nm (2.0 kgf/m, 14 lbf/ft)

Apply engine oil to the bolt threads.

6 mm bolts:12 Nm (1.2 kgf/m, 8.7 lbf/ft)

Apply engine oil to the bolt threads.

6 mm bolts:(11), (12), (13), (14)

 Install the cylinder head in the reverse order of removal:
  1. Clean the cylinder head and block surface.
  2. Install the cylinder head gasket (A) and dowel pins (B) on the cylinder block. Always use a new cylinder head gasket.

  1. Set the crankshaft to TDC. Align the TDC mark (A) on the timing belt drive pulley with the pointer (B) on the oil pump.
