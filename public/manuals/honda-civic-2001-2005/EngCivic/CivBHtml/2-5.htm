Accurate Inspection of Damaged Parts (Visual)

Measurement

 2-5  

Whenever possible, make judgements and conclusions based on measurements.

  • Measure the wheel alignment to prevent uneven tyre wear or incorrect steering wheel alignment.
  • If there are any deviations, use a tram-tracking gauge and measure the body parts.

  • If there is any twisting to the body, measure with a frame centring gauge.
  • To measure body dimensions, use a universal tram gauge and convex tool.
