Fuel Filter Replacement

  1. Place a suitable container under the fuel filter to catch the water.
  1. Loosen the water screw (A) at the underside of the fuel filter until water drops.

  1. Drain about 85 ml (2.9 fl.oz, 3.0 lmp.oz) to 150 ml (5.1 fl.oz, 5.3 lmp.oz) of water.
  1. Tighten the water screw securely.
  1. Bleed the fuel system (see page 11-157).

  1. Disconnect the hoses from the fuel filter (A).

  1. Remove the bolts (B) from the fuel filter bracket (C).
  1. Remove the fuel filter (A).

  1. Install the part in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Bleed the fuel system (see page 11-157).
