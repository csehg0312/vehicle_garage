Oil pressure Switch Replacement

  1. Remove the oil filter housing cover, then separate the oil filter housing cover and oil filter element.
  1. Install the new O-rings (A) and oil filter element (B) to the oil filter housing cover (C).

  1. Install the oil filter housing cover to the oil filter housing.

  1. Disconnect the mass air flow (MAF) sensor connector (A) and remove the vacuum hose (B), then remove the intake air duct (C) and air cleaner housing (D).

  1. Disconnect the oil pressure switch harness, then remove the oil pressure switch.

  1. Apply liquid gasket to the oil pressure switch threads, then install the oil pressure switch.
