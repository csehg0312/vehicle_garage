Manual Transmission

Output Shaft Disassembly

 13-23  

Special Tools Required

  • Bearing remover 5-8840-0012-0 (J-22227-A)
  • Bearing remover 5-8840-0015-0 (J-22912-01)

  1. Remove the front output shaft bearing (A) using the special tool and a suitable short bar (B) with a press.

  1. Remove the rear output shaft bearing (A) and 3rd and 4th output gear (B) together using the special tool with a press.

  1. Remove the 2nd gear needle bearing, 2nd gear needle bearing collar, output 2nd gear, 2nd block ring set, 1st and 2nd synchroniser assembly, 1st block ring set, output 1st gear, 3rd and 4th gear feather key and 1st gear needle bearing, using a press.

  1. Remove the 1st thrust bearing.
  1. Remove the output shaft.
