Intake Manifold and Exhaust System

Intake Manifold and EGR Valve Removal

 9-2  

  1. Remove the acoustic cover.

  1. Remove the air cleaner housing (see step 6 on page 5-2).
  1. Remove the intake manifold pipe.
  1. Remove the EGR valve.
  1. Remove the EGR pipe.
  1. Remove the oil filter assembly (see page 8-5).
  1. Remove the four injection pipes (see page 11-160).
  1. Remove the three bolts (A) securing the engine wire harness, three bolts (B) securing the hose bracket, then disconnect the fuel leak rubber hose (C) and vacuum hose (D).

  1. Remove the bolts (A) and nuts (B) then remove the intake manifold assembly (C).
