Cooling System

Water Pump and Thermostat Inspection (cont'd)

 10-9  

Water Pump Removal

  1. Drain coolant-collect coolant.
  1. Loosen fastening bolts (A) before removing drive-belt (B).

  1. Remove drive-belt.
  1. Remove the water pump assembly (C) and gasket (D).

Water Pump Installation

  1. Install the water pump assembly (A) with a new gasket (B) to the cylinder block.

  1. Tighten fastening bolts (C) to the specified torque.

Tightening Torque:

24 Nm (2.4 kgf/m, 17.4 lbf/ft).

  1. Install the water pump pulley (D) to the water pump. Install the drive-belt (E).
  1. Tighten fastening bolts (F) to the specified torque.

Tightening Torque:

9.8 Nm (1.0 kgf/m, 7.2 lbf/ft).
