Is about 250 kPa (1,877 mmHg, 73 in. Hg) indicated?

YES – Replace the MAP sensor.

NO – Go to step 12.

  1. Disconnect ECM connector.
  1. Check the MAP sensor signal circuit, +5 V circuit and ground circuit for open/short to ground.
