Charging system

Alternator Replacement (cont'd)

 4-22  

  1. Disconnect the alternator connector (A) and WHT wire (B) from the alternator.

  1. Remove the oil feed hose (C), vacuum pump return hose (D) and vacuum hose (E).
  1. Remove the two bolts securing the alternator.

  1. Remove the alternator.
  1. Install the alternator and drive belt in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Install the bulkhead in the reverse order of removal. Apply body paint to the bulkhead mounting bolts.
