Conventional Brake Components

Master Cylinder Removal/Installation (cont'd)

 19-10  

  1. Install the master cylinder in the reverse order of removal and note these items:
  • Replace all the rubber parts with new ones whenever the master cylinder is removed.
  • Check the pushrod clearance before installing the master cylinder and adjust it if necessary.
  • Use a new rod seal on reassembly.
  • Coat the inner bore lip (A) and outer circumference (B) of the new rod seal with the recommended seal grease in the master cylinder set.
  • Install the rod seal onto the master cylinder with its grooved side (C) toward the master cylinder.
  • Check the brake pedal height and free play after installing the master cylinder and adjust it if necessary.
