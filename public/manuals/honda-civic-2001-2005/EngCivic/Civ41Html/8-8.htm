Oil Pump Reassembly

  1. Install the outer rotor (A) into the cylinder block.

  1. Install the inner rotor (B) into the outer rotor.
  1. Install the oil pump retainer (C) on the cylinder block.
  1. Clean sealing surfaces and remove liquid gasket residue.
  1. Coat inner and outer rotor with oil and insert in cylinder block.
  1. Insert new gasket (A) in groove in oil pump

  1. Apply a bead of liquid gasket to sealing surface (crosshatched) of oil pump retainer.

    Install oil pump retainer (A) to cylinder block.

Tightening torque;

9.8 Nm (1.0 kgf/m, 7.2 lbf/ft)
