Charging System

Alternator Replacement

 4-21  

  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery first, then the positive cable.
  1. Remove the drive belt (see page 4-20).
  1. Disconnect the fan motor connectors (A), hood switch connector (B) and compressor clutch connector (C), then remove the harness clamps (D).

  1. Remove the condenser brackets (A) and the upper bracket and cushions (B), then remove the bulkhead (C).

  1. Remove the two bolts and loosen the two bolts securing the condenser fan shroud, then remove the condenser fan shroud.

  1. Remove the turbocharger cover.
