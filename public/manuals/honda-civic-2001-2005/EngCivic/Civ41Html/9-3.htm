Intake Manifold and Exhaust System

Intake Manifold and EGR Valve Inspection

 9-3

Intake Manifold and EGR Valve Installation

  1. Connect the vacuum pump and gauge to the actuator of EGR valve assembly.

  1. Apply 0 kPa to 25 kPa (0.25 kgf/cm2, 1.8 lbf/ft) vacuum and check EGR valve moving condition.
  1. If there is abnormal moving conditions on the EGR valve, replace the EGR valve assembly.
  1. Inspect VSS (Variable Swirl System) for damage or any other problems and push the valve with your finger to inspect normal condition or valve sticking.
  1. Inspect the EGR valve for damage or any other problems.

  1. Apply liquid gasket to the VSS.
  1. Install the intake manifold assembly with gasket to cylinder head.

    Tighten the fixing bolts and nuts with the specified torque.

Tightening torque:

24.5 Nm (2.5 kgf/m, 18 lbf/ft)

  1. Connect the vacuum hose, fuel rubber hose and install the hose bracket and engine wire harness with specified torque.

    Tightening torque:

7.4 Nm (0.75 kgf/m, 5.4 lbf/ft)

  1. Install the four injection pipes (see page 11-160).
  1. Install the oil filter assembly (see page 8-5).
  1. Install the EGR valve assembly with specified torque.

    Tightening torque:

24.5 Nm (2.5 kgf/m, 18 lbf/ft)

  1. Install the EGR pipe and gasket with specified torque.

    Tightening torque:

24.5 Nm (2.5 kgf/m, 18 lbf/ft)

  1. Install the intake manifold pipe with specified torque.

    Tightening torque:

24.5 Nm (2.5 kgf/m, 18 lbf/ft)

  1. Install the air cleaner housing (see step 6 on page 5-2).
  1. Install the acoustic cover with specified torque.

    Tightening torque:

7.4 Nm (0.75 kgf/m, 5.4 lbf/ft)
