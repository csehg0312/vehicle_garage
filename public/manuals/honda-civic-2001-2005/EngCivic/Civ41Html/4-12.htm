Starting System

Starter Overhaul (cont'd)

 4-12  

  1. Measure the brush length. Replace the brushes as a set if one more of the brush length is less than limit.

Brush length:

Standard:

15.5 mm (0.6102 in.)

Limit:

10.5 mm (0.4134in.)

  1. Use a circuit tester to inspect the brush holder insulation. Touch one probe to the holder plate and the other probe to positive brush holder.

    There should be no continuity.

  1. Use a circuit tester to inspect the magnetic switch continuity. Touch one probe to the S connector and the other probe to the magnetic switch body. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, replace the magnetic switch assembly.

  1. Use a circuit tester to inspect the magnetic switch continuity. Touch one probe to the S connector and the other probe to the M connector. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, replace the magnetic switch assembly.
