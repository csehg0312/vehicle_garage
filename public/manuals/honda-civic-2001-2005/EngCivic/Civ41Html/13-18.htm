Manual Transmission

Transmission Disassembly (cont'd)

 13-18  

  1. Remove the 5th gear needle bearing collar (A) and 5th gear thrust washer (B), using the special tools.

  1. Remove the bearing ring.
  1. Remove the 10 X 1.25 reverse idler shaft bolt and washer.
  1. Remove the two (10 X 1.5 mm) flange bolts and 12 (10 X 1.5 mm) bolts and separate the transmission case from clutch housing.
  1. Remove the reverse idler gear (A) and reverse idler shaft (B).

  1. Remove the reverse detent plug (A), the reverse detent spring and reverse detent ball.

  1. Lift the 5th shift rod (A). With the detent aligned facing the same way, remove 5th (A) and reverse (B) shift rods and blocks assembly at the same time.
