Camshaft Bracket Installation

  1. Install camshaft bearing brackets 1 - 4 on camshaft carrier - note identification marks - arrows on bearing brackets point towards engine front side.
  1. Remove setting pin from exhaust camshaft gear and install camshaft bearing bracket 5-moderately tighten fastening nuts.

    Tighten camshaft bearing brackets in sequence illustrated ((1)) – ((13)) in steps of 1/2 to 1 turn.

Tightening torque:

(M8 Nut):

22 Nm (2.2 kgf/m, 16 lbf/ft)

(M10 Nut):

43 Nm (4.4 kgf/m, 32 lbf/ft)

Camshaft Oil Seal Installation

  1. Install camshaft oil seal using special tool (5-8840-9051-0).
