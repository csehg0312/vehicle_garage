Water Glow Plug Test

 10-7

Water Glow Plug Replacement

  1. Remove the water glow plug harness (A) from the water glow plug.

  1. Measure resistance between the positive terminal (A) and body ground.

Resistance:

Green colour (200W):

0.91 ohms

Silver colour (300W):

0.61 ohms

  1. Disconnect the mass air flow (MAF) sensor connector (A) and remove the vacuum hose (B), then remove the intake air duct (C) and air cleaner housing (D).

  1. Remove the water glow plug harness (A), then remove the water glow plug (B).

  1. Install the water glow plug in the reverse order of removal.
