PCV Valve Inspection and Test

 11-132  

PCV Valve Replacement

  1. Check the PCV valve (A), hoses (B) and connections for leaks or restrictions.

  1. At idle, make sure there is a clicking sound from the PCV valve when the hose between the PCV valve and intake manifold is lightly pinched (A) with your fingers or pliers.

    If there is no clicking sound, check the PCV valve grommet for cracks or damage. If the grommet is OK, replace the PCV valve and recheck.

  1. Disconnect the PCV hose.
  1. Remove the PCV valve (A).

  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal with a new O-ring (B).
