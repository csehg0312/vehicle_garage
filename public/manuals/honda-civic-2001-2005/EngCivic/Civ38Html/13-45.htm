Mainshaft Bearing and Oil Seal Replacement (cont'd)

 13-45  

Countershaft Bearing Replacement

  1. Drive the new oil seal in from the transmission side using the special tools.

  1. Drive the new ball bearing (A) in from the transmission side using the special tools.

Special Tools Required

  • Oil seal driver, 07JAD-PL90100
  • Adjustable bearing puller, 20 - 40 mm, 07JAC-PH80100
  • Slide hammer, 07741-0010201
  • Bearing remover shaft, 07JAC-PH80200

  1. Remove the bearing set plate (A) from the clutch housing (B).

  1. Remove the needle bearing (A) using the special tools, then remove the oil guide plate C (B).
