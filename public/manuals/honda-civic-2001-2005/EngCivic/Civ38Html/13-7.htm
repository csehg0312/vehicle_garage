Manual Transmission

Transmission Removal (cont'd)

 13-7  

  1. Raise vehicle and make sure it is securely supported.
  1. Drain the transmission fluid. Reinstall the drain bolt using a new washer (see page 13-4).
  1. Remove the splash shield.

  1. Remove the Three Way Catalytic Converter (TWC) assembly (see step 25 on page 5-6).
  1. Remove the driveshafts (see page 16-3).
  1. Remove the intermediate shaft (see page 16-18).
  1. Remove the front engine mount bracket mounting bolt.

  1. Remove the three bolts securing the transmission rear mount.

  1. Support the sub-frame with a 4 x 4 x 40 in. piece of wood and a jack.
