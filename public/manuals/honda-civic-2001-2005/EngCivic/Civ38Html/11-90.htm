PGM-FI System

Injector Replacement

 11-90  

  1. Relieve fuel pressure (see page 11-115).
  1. Remove the engine cover.
  1. Disconnect the connectors from the injectors (A), ground cable (B) and bracket (C).

  1. Disconnect the quick-connect fittings (D).
  1. Remove the fuel rail mounting nuts (E) from the fuel rail (F).
  1. Remove the injector clip (G) from the injector.
  1. Remove the injector from the fuel rail.
