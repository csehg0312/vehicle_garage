PGM-FI System

DTC Troubleshooting (cont'd)

 11-76  

  1. Measure voltage between ECM connector terminal A26 and body ground.

ECM CONNECTOR A (31P)

Wire side of female terminals

Is there about 5 V?

YES - Repair open in the wire between the ECM (A26) and TDC sensor.

NO - Go to step 11.

  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect ECM connector A (31P).
  1. Check for continuity between ECM connector terminal A26 and body ground.

ECM CONNECTOR A (31P)

Wire side of female terminals

Is there continuity?

YES - Repair short in the wire between the ECM (A26) and the TDC sensor.

NO - Substitute a known-good ECM and recheck (see page 11-5). If symptom/indication goes away, replace the original ECM.
