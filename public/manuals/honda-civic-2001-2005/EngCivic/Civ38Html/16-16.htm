Driveshafts Installation

  1. Close the ear portion (A) of the band with a commercially available boot band pincers (B).

  1. Check the clearance between the closed ear portion of the bands. If the clearance is not within the standard, close the ear portion of the bands further.

  1. 6.0 mm (0.24 in.) MAX
  2. 3.0 mm (0.12in. ) MAX

  1. Install a new set ring onto the set ring groove of the driveshaft (left driveshaft).

  1. Apply 0.5 - 1.0 g (0.02 - 0.04 oz) of specified grease to the whole splined surface (A) of the right driveshaft. After applying grease, remove the grease from the splined grooves at intervals of 2 - 3 splines and from the set ring groove (B) so that air can bleed from the intermediate shaft.
