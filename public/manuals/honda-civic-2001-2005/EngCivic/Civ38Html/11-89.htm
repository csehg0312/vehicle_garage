  1. Disconnect the ECM connector E (31P) and the ABS control unit 31P connector. Make sure the PGM Tester is disconnected from the DLC.
  1. Check for continuity between DLC terminal No. 7 and body ground.

DATA LINK CONNECTOR (DLC)

Terminal side of male terminals

Is there continuity?

YES - Repair short in the wire between the DLC terminal No. 7 and the ECM (E23).

NO - Go to step 9.

  1. Check for continuity between DLC terminal No. 7 and ECM terminal E23.

Is there continuity?

YES - Substitute a known-good ECM and recheck (see page 11-5). If symptom/indication goes away with a known-good ECM, replace the original ECM.

NO - Repair open in the wire between DLC terminal No. 7 and the ECM (E23).
