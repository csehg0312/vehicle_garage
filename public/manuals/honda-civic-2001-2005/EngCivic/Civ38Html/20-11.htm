Exterior Trim

Tailgate Spoiler Replacement

 20-11  

NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.

  1. Remove the tailgate upper trim, refer to the 2001 Civic Shop Manual Supplement, P/N 62S5A22 (see page 20-32).
  1. From inside the tailgate, pull the high mount brake light connector (A) out from the hole in the tailgate and disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the bolts (B) and nuts (C) securing the tailgate spoiler (D).

  1. Pull up the tailgate spoiler (A) and slip the high mount brake light connector (B) through the hole (C) in the tailgate, then remove the spoiler.

  1. Install the spoiler in the reverse order of removal and make sure the high mount brake light connector is plugged in properly.
