VTEC/VTC System

DTC Troubleshooting (cont'd)

 11-100  

  1. Connect the ECM connector terminal A5 and B1 with a jumper wire for below 1 minute.

    NOTE: Do not jump for above 1 minute.

ECM CONNECTORS

Wire side of female terminals

Did the engine stall or run rough?

YES - Test-drive at a steady speed between 30 - 60 km/h (19 - 37 mph) for 10 minutes.

If temporary DTC P0011 is indicated, substitute a known-good ECM and recheck (see page 11-5), replace the original ECM.

NO - Go to step 15.

  1. Check the VTC actuator (see page 6-9).

    Is the VTC actuator OK?

YES - Remove the auto-tensioner (see page 4-26) and replace the VTC oil filter. Substitute a known-good ECM and recheck (see page 11-5). If symptom/indication goes away, replace the original ECM.

NO - Replace the VTC actuator.
