Oil Pressure Switch Replacement

  1. Install the oil pump chain tensioner.

  1. Remove the set clip from the oil pump chain tensioner.

  1. Install the oil pan (see page 7-28).

  1. Disconnect the oil pressure switch connector, then remove the oil pressure switch.

  1. Apply liquid gasket to the oil pressure switch threads, then install the oil pressure switch.
