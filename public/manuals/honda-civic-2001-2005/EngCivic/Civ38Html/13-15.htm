Manual Transmission

Transmission Installation (cont'd)

 13-15  

  1. Connect the back-up light switch connector (A), the vehicle speed sensor (VSS) connector (B), and the reverse lockout solenoid connector (C).

  1. Connect the transmission ground cable (A).

  1. Install the battery tray (B).

  1. Install the intake air duct (see step 31 on page 5-16).
  1. Install the air cleaner (see page 11-126).
  1. Install the intake manifold cover (see step 5 on page 5-3).
  1. Install the battery. Connect the positive (+) cable first, then the negative (-) cable to the battery.
  1. Refill the transmission fluid (see page 13-4).
  1. Test-drive the vehicle.
  1. Check the clutch operation.
  1. Check the front wheel alignment, refer to the 2001 Civic Shop Manual (see page 18-4).
  1. Enter the anti-theft code for the radio, then enter the customer's radio station presets.
