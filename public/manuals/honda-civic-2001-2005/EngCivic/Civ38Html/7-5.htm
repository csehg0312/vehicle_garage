Engine Block

Flywheel Removal and Installation

 7-5  

Special Tools Required

Ring gear holder 07LAB-PV00100

  1. Remove the transmission (see page 13-5).
  1. Remove the eight flywheel bolts, then separate the flywheel from the crankshaft flange. After installation, tighten the bolts in a criss-cross pattern.

  1. Inspect ring gear teeth for wear or damage

  1. Install the transmission (see page 13-10).
