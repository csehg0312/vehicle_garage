System Description (cont'd)

 14-54  

Hydraulic Flow (cont'd)

Position

Line pressure (3) passed through the manual valve flows to the 1-2 shift valve and changed to line pressure (3').

Line pressure (3') pushes the servo valve to the reverse/park position, passes through the servo valve and flows to the manual valve. Line pressure (3') is intercepted at the manual valve and hydraulic pressure is not applied to the clutches.

NOTE: When used, ''left'' or ''right'' indicates direction on the hydraulic circuit.
