Cylinder Head

Cylinder Head Removal (cont'd)

 6-10  

  1. Remove the ground cable (A), upper radiator hose (B), lower radiator hose (C), heater hose (D) and water bypass hose (E).

  1. Remove the two bolts securing the connecting pipe, then remove the connecting pipe (B) from the water passage.

  1. Remove the water bypass hose (B).
  1. Remove the alternator, refer to the '01 Civic Shop manual on this CD (see page 4-31).
  2. Remove the air conditioning (A/C) hose bracket (A) and P/S hose bracket (B).

  1. Remove the engine wire harness connectors and wire harness clamps from the intake manifold.
  • Idle air control (IAC) valve connector
  • Throttle position sensor connector
  • Manifold absolute pressure (MAP) sensor connector
  • Evaporative emission (EVAP) canister purge valve connector (D14Z6, D16V1 engines)
  • Engine coolant temperature (ECT) sensor connector
  • Radiator fan switch connector
  • CKP sensor connector
  • TDC sensor connector
  • Exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) connector (D14Z6, D16V1 engines)
  • VTEC solenoid valve connector (D16V1, D16V3 engines)
  • VTEC oil pressure switch connector (D16V1 engine)
  • Oil pressure sensor connector
