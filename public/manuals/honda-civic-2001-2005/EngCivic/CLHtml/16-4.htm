Driveshaft Reassembly (cont'd)

 16-4  

  1. Pack the inboard joint with the joint grease included in the new driveshaft set.

Grease quantity

Inboard joint:

D14Z6 engine models:

Right driveshaft:

108-126 g (3.8-4.4 oz)

Left driveshaft:

103.5-121.5 g (3.7-4.3 oz)

D16V1 engine models:

100-110 g (3.5-3.9 oz)

  1. Fit the inboard joint onto the driveshaft and note these items:
  • Reinstall the inboard joint onto the driveshaft by aligning the marks (A) on the inboard joint and the rollers.
  • Hold the driveshaft so the inboard joint points up to prevent it from falling off.
  1. Adjust the length of the driveshafts to the figure below, then adjust the boots to halfway between full compression and full extension. Make sure the ends of the boots seat in the grooves of the driveshaft and joint.

Right driveshaft:

498.7 – 503.7 mm (19.6 – 19.8 in.)
